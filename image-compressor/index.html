Image format compression guide

Which format to choose for maximum compression with the best quality.

Format Type Typical reduction Best for Browser support
AVIF Both 50–90% Photos, web images — best modern format Chrome, Firefox, Edge (No IE)
WebP Both 25–80% General web use, replaces JPG + PNG All modern browsers
JPG / JPEG Lossy 40–75% Photos, gradients, complex images Universal
PNG Lossless 10–40% Logos, illustrations, transparency needed Universal
GIF Lossless 5–30% Simple animations, icons (256 colors max) Universal
BMP Lossless Convert away Windows legacy — convert to PNG/WebP Universal
SVG Lossless 30–60% (minify) Icons, logos, vector graphics All modern browsers

Frequently asked questions

Is this image compressor really free?
Yes — 100% free with no limits. You can compress as many images as you need, in any session, with no account required.
Does it compress images without losing quality?
The compressor uses smart lossy and lossless algorithms. You choose the quality level — at 80% quality, most images look identical to the original while shrinking by 60–80%.
What image formats are supported?
You can compress JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF (static), BMP, and TIFF. You can also convert between formats during compression — e.g. PNG → WebP.
Is my image uploaded to a server?
No. All compression happens in your browser using the Canvas API and WebAssembly codecs. Your images never leave your device.
Can I compress multiple images at once?
Yes — drag and drop up to 20 images at once and they're all compressed in parallel. Download them individually or as a single ZIP file.
What's the maximum file size I can compress?
There's no server-side limit because everything runs in your browser. In practice, images up to 50 MB compress smoothly. Extremely large RAW files may be slower on older devices.
How much will my file size be reduced?
Results vary by image and format, but typical reductions are 50–80% for JPEGs, 40–70% for PNGs, and up to 90% when converting to modern formats like WebP or AVIF.
