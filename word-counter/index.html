Frequently asked questions

How do I count words online?
Paste or type your text into the word counter text box above. The word count, character count, sentence count, paragraph count, and reading time update instantly as you type — no button to click.
What counts as a word?
A word is any sequence of characters separated by whitespace (spaces, tabs, or newlines). Hyphenated words like 'self-aware' count as one word. Numbers count as words. Punctuation attached to a word does not create separate words.
What is the difference between characters with and without spaces?
Characters with spaces includes every character in the text, including spaces and newlines. Characters without spaces counts only the non-whitespace characters — letters, numbers, and punctuation. The without-spaces count is used for platform character limits in tweets, SMS, and some publishing platforms.
How is reading time calculated?
Reading time is estimated at 230 words per minute, which is the average adult reading speed for on-screen text. Speaking time is estimated at 130 words per minute, which is an average natural pace for presentations. Both are estimates and may vary based on content complexity and individual speed.
What is a good word count for a blog post?
For SEO, most experts recommend 1,500–3,000 words for a blog post targeting competitive keywords. Longer posts tend to rank better because they cover a topic comprehensively. Short posts (300–600 words) work well for news, updates, or simple how-to guides. The ideal length depends on the topic and competition.
How many words is a 5-minute speech?
At an average speaking pace of 130 words per minute, a 5-minute speech is approximately 650 words. At a faster pace of 150 wpm it's about 750 words, and at a slower 100 wpm it's about 500 words. The word counter shows estimated speaking time for your specific text.
What are the Twitter and LinkedIn character limits?
Twitter (X) allows up to 280 characters per tweet for standard users. LinkedIn posts support up to 3,000 characters. Instagram captions allow 2,200 characters. SMS messages are 160 characters per message. The platform limit tracker above updates in real time as you type.
