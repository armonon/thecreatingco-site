AFTER HOURS / Form Studio / Web editor

A small change. A different presence.

An editable clasp study, from its first proportions to a broader frame and two contrasting metal finishes.

Start with proportion

The buckle starter establishes a 55 × 38 mm outer profile. Its frame, openings and centre bar remain in the editable feature history.

Widen the outer rail

Changing the outer width to 64 mm preserves the openings and gives the right rail more weight. The geometry visibly updates in the same isometric view.

Bring in warmth

Polished brass changes the surface to a warmer finish, with roughness at 0.17 and metal at 0.94. The camera and geometry stay fixed for comparison.

Keep a cooler option

Brushed steel offers a cooler alternative. The finished state was saved as an editable Form project and exported to a verified PNG.

Actual browser session. The starter’s optional fillet failed and was suppressed; these views show the resulting rectangular geometry. This is a visual study, not manufacturing or fit validation.

AFTER HOURS / Luma Studio / Web editor

An image becomes an invitation.

Build a real exhibition poster: one original still life, two editable text elements, and a controlled shift in tone.

Begin with an image

An original AI-generated still life becomes a 1024 × 1536 portrait document. The source artwork remains its own raster layer.

Give it a voice

AFTER HOURS and OBJECTS / SOUND / IDEAS are separate editable text elements, placed in the open space around the still life.

Develop the atmosphere

A tone layer sets exposure to 0.20, contrast to 12, shadows to 8 and temperature to −8. The before and after views use the same composition.

Keep every decision

The four-layer document was saved in Luma’s native format. Its exported PNG was opened and visually checked at the original 1024 × 1536 size.

AFTER HOURS / StemDeck / Earlier browser companion

Four parts. One evening mix.

Start with original percussion, bass, harmony and melody. Set their entry point, balance their levels, and render a short mix you can listen to.

Hear the ingredients

Four supplied original parts fill the four decks. Each 18.5-second stereo file has its own real waveform; these parts were composed separately.

Balance the arrangement

Deck levels are 60%, 50%, 60% and 80%. All four cues start at 4.615 seconds, about two bars into the source, with whole-track looping enabled.

Bring the melody forward

While the loaded decks play, the crossfader moves from the centre to 0.40 toward the harmony and melody decks.

Keep the sketch

The browser renders an 18-second WAV from those cue, level, loop and crossfade settings. Playback was stopped after the listening check.

Open StemDeck

Listen to the Evening Mix

The actual 18-second StemDeck output. Press Play to listen; playback starts only when you choose it.

Download the Evening Mix WAV

Actual browser companion. The exported stereo, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit WAV was decoded and checked for non-silent audio and clipping. Export uses the current settings; it does not record live fader moves. No source separation or native plugins are implied.

AFTER HOURS / ScenePilot / Browser Lite

An evening, in eighteen seconds.

Bring three original visual studies and a freshly mixed soundtrack into an editable cinematic sequence.

Gather the evening

Three original five-second clips bring the artwork, jacket and Form clasp into the media bin. The real StemDeck mix supplies the 18-second soundtrack.

Find the first cut

Cinematic mode analyzes the soundtrack at 104 BPM and creates ten master clips, with five additional overlay placements. The sequence stays editable.

Soften each arrival

The incoming edit changes from Zoom to a 0.30-second Dissolve, then applies to every master edit. The program monitor shows the resulting layered picture.

Return to the timeline

After saving and reloading, Restore saved project brings back the three source clips, soundtrack, 15 timeline items and effects. A real exported cut JSON preserves the edit map.

Open ScenePilot
Download the editable cut mapDownload the original source bundle

Actual Browser Lite session. Source clips use original artwork, jacket references and the Form render. The downloadable JSON contains edit metadata and requires the source files. MP4 export canceled before writing a file; no rendered video is claimed.

AFTER HOURS / Ghost Studio / Web studio

Two references. A form to explore.

Bring a coherent front and back pair into a new collection, change the silhouette, and inspect the resulting jacket from both sides.

Start with both sides

An original AI-generated front and back pair establishes the midnight-blue jacket and amber collar. The source comparison keeps both references available.

Give the silhouette room

Changing Natural to Relaxed produces a broader form. The wool setting and stiffness of 50 remain in place while the app settles the drape.

Look behind the form

The rear view exposes the back construction beside the supplied reference. The result remains an estimated visual study.

Keep the model and its source

The selected jacket exported to a textured GLB. The editable collection also retains the two original reference images and relaxed settings.

Open Ghost Studio
Download the jacket GLBDownload the editable collection

Actual browser session. The GLB contains five meshes; the editable archive passed integrity checks and its two images match the originals. The model has visibly stepped outline edges. Unseen sides and dimensions are estimates; this is not sewing-pattern or fit validation.

Return to the shelf

Previous project06 / 08Next: Reclaim

AFTER HOURS / Reclaim / File review · read-only

Find the copies worth reviewing.

An exhibition folder contains working files, exports, backups, and two notes with the same name. Compare their actual content before making a decision.

See the selected set

Seven original demonstration files total about 6.3 MB. The review lists their names and sizes and finds two groups of identical content.

Match the bytes

A PDF pair and an audio pair match despite their different filenames. Together, the extra copies account for 3,277,228 bytes; no files were deleted.

Narrow the review

Searching Field-Notes isolates the working PDF and its final-copy counterpart while preserving the full review.

Keep the evidence

Two 67-byte notes.txt files have different contents and are not grouped as duplicates. The downloaded JSON report records the comparison results.

Open Reclaim
Download the actual review report

Actual read-only browser review of disposable fixtures. The downloaded report was parsed against all seven original names, sizes and available hashes. Every source hash remained unchanged. This is selected-file review, not a system scan or a claim of reclaimed disk space.

Previous project07 / 08Next: Bounce

AFTER HOURS / Bounce / Local music player

A session worth returning to.

Bring the four original parts and the finished mix into one listening queue. Shape the sound, choose an order, and save it on this device.

Begin with the finished mix

The verified StemDeck output plays alongside its four original source parts in a new five-track collection.

Choose a passage

Bass moves to +1 dB, mid to 0 dB and treble to +4 dB. Seeking to about eight seconds picks out a passage in the 18-second mix.

Find a part

A search for Harmony isolates the matching track. The queue was arranged to move from the complete mix through harmony, melody, bass and percussion.

Return to After Hours

The library is saved as After Hours. After reload, all five tracks return in the chosen order, with the playlist name and EQ settings retained.

Open Bounce

The mix in this session

The original StemDeck output used in the playlist. This download does not bake in Bounce’s listening EQ.

Download the playlist file list

Actual browser companion. The exported JSON is a playlist file list with filenames and sizes; audio is not embedded in that file. Saved audio copies remain local to this browser and can be cleared or evicted. Original source files were retained.
