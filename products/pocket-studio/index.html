Pocket Studio: Voice Memos & Recording in The Circle

Pocket Studio is The Circle’s local audio recorder for voice memos, melodies and sounds. It is distinct from Sattari Pocket, the beat sketchpad.

Open Pocket Studio ↗

Start with a small project

  1. Open the Pocket Studio view in The Circle. Allow microphone access only when you are ready to record.
  2. Record a short test and listen back before keeping a longer memo. Keep a separate copy of recordings you want to retain.
  3. Sign in if you want to upload a recording. Check the sharing context and The Circle’s privacy notes before posting.

Current limits

Public alpha. Recording uploads require sign-in. Local recordings and uploaded media have different storage and deletion boundaries; see the privacy notes.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Open Pocket Studio and check microphone permission
  2. Make a short test recording and listen back
  3. Review the recording before uploading or keeping a copy

Before you rely on it

Recording uploads require sign-in. Check microphone and browser behavior first and avoid recording people without their consent.
