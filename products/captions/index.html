A GOOD PLACE TO BEGIN

One tool. Three starting points.

1 / 3

A founder introduction

Make a short spoken introduction easier to follow.

  1. Import a short video with clear speech, or use an existing SRT/VTT file.
  2. Review names and punctuation, then choose a readable caption style.
  3. Export the subtitle file and, if needed, a burned-in WebM.

An audio-only announcement

Give a voice note a visual format.

  1. Import an audio clip and check the transcript.
  2. Choose an audiogram aspect ratio and adjust caption grouping.
  3. Keep the tab visible during the real-time video export.

Restyle existing subtitles

Try a new presentation without retranscribing the clip.

  1. Import your SRT or VTT alongside the matching media.
  2. Compare styles and check contrast against the footage.
  3. Export updated subtitles or a styled WebM, then review the result.

What should I know before starting?

The on-device speech model can mishear names, accents and sung lyrics. Review every transcript. Burn-in runs in real time with the tab visible. Translation, dubbing and universal language accuracy are not promised.

How do I keep my work?

Download SRT/VTT to preserve the edited words and timings. Keep the original clip and inspect the exported video.

Can I download a native app?

This page opens the public web alpha. There are no public native downloads yet. Check current availability.
