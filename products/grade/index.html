What should I know before starting?

This is color transfer, not relighting or skin-aware grading. It assumes Rec.709/sRGB, not log or HDR. Histogram mode can posterize flat references. Cross-editor round-trips are not broadly qualified.

Grade · alpha

Reference Color Transfer & LUT Tool

Compare an image with a color reference and adjust the strength of a transferred look. Grade helps explore a direction before final grading.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Choose a source and reference image
  2. Adjust transfer strength and compare the split preview
  3. Export a graded PNG or .cube LUT and test it in the destination editor

Before you rely on it

Color transfer is not relighting or skin-aware grading. The workflow targets sRGB/Rec.709, not log or HDR material.

Declared input types: image/png, image/jpeg, image/webp, video/*, .cube

Declared inputs describe expected formats, not certified decoder coverage. Save a separate copy of important work.

How to evaluate a colour reference before making a LUT

Colour transfer can change a picture dramatically while damaging the tones that matter. Compare neutral patches and important subjects before exporting a look.
