Jewelry Casting Checks Preview

Review a jewelry model before discussing it with a caster. Cast is a focused preview inside the Form review studio.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Open the Form review studio and choose Open Cast from its menu
  2. Bring in a supported STL, GLB or OBJ model
  3. Inspect the available checks and discuss the model with your manufacturer

Before you rely on it

A preview is not manufacturing certification. Material, process and final casting approval belong with your manufacturer.

Where to start: Form review studio menu > Open Cast. Preview, not manufacturing certification.

Declared input types: model/stl, model/gltf-binary, model/obj, .stl, .glb, .obj

Declared inputs describe expected formats, not certified decoder coverage. Save a separate copy of important work.
