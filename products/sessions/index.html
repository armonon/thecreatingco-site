Sessions: Shared Listening Rooms in The Circle

Sessions is the shared listening experience in The Circle. Open the Sessions view to explore rooms and listen with other people; it is separate from StemDeck’s remix workspace.

Open Sessions ↗

Start with a small project

  1. Open Sessions in The Circle and choose the listening room you want to join. Guests can join Sessions.
  2. Read the room’s visibility and participation details before sharing anything. An unlisted room should not be treated as private.
  3. For music you want to edit yourself, use StemDeck. For local-file listening without a shared room, use Bounce.

Current limits

Public alpha. Accounts, shared media and room visibility follow The Circle’s privacy policy. Do not upload recordings or music without the rights to share them.

Shared Creative Sessions

Join a shared room for creative work and conversation. Sessions is a focused view inside The Circle rather than a separate editing app.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Open the Sessions view in The Circle
  2. Choose or join the shared room appropriate for your work
  3. Review what you are sharing before bringing in media

Before you rely on it

An unlisted room is not a private room. Only share media you have permission to use and review the room’s visibility.

Declared input types: Start in the workspace; this catalog does not declare file import types.

Declared inputs describe expected formats, not certified decoder coverage. Save a separate copy of important work.
