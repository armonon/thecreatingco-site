AFTER HOURS / Bounce / Local music player

A session worth returning to.

Bring the four original parts and the finished mix into one listening queue. Shape the sound, choose an order, and save it on this device.

Begin with the finished mix

The verified StemDeck output plays alongside its four original source parts in a new five-track collection.

Choose a passage

Bass moves to +1 dB, mid to 0 dB and treble to +4 dB. Seeking to about eight seconds picks out a passage in the 18-second mix.

Find a part

A search for Harmony isolates the matching track. The queue was arranged to move from the complete mix through harmony, melody, bass and percussion.

Return to After Hours

The library is saved as After Hours. After reload, all five tracks return in the chosen order, with the playlist name and EQ settings retained.

The mix in this session

The original StemDeck output used in the playlist. This download does not bake in Bounce’s listening EQ.

Download the playlist file list

Actual browser companion. The exported JSON is a playlist file list with filenames and sizes; audio is not embedded in that file. Saved audio copies remain local to this browser and can be cleared or evicted. Original source files were retained.

YOUR BROWSER EDITION

Your web edition.

Play your audio, search a local queue, adjust EQ, and save a playlist in this browser.

Current limitations

Browser codecs determine which files play. Files and playlists stay in this browser; storage may be cleared. This companion does not include the native tag reader, desktop audio engine or a streaming catalog.
