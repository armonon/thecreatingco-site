AFTER HOURS / Form Studio / Web editor

A small change. A different presence.

An editable clasp study, from its first proportions to a broader frame and two contrasting metal finishes.

Start with proportion

The buckle starter establishes a 55 × 38 mm outer profile. Its frame, openings and centre bar remain in the editable feature history.

Widen the outer rail

Changing the outer width to 64 mm preserves the openings and gives the right rail more weight. The geometry visibly updates in the same isometric view.

Bring in warmth

Polished brass changes the surface to a warmer finish, with roughness at 0.17 and metal at 0.94. The camera and geometry stay fixed for comparison.

Keep a cooler option

Brushed steel offers a cooler alternative. The finished state was saved as an editable Form project and exported to a verified PNG.

Form · alpha

Browser 3D Design & CAD

Turn a sketch or an imported model into a 3D study. Form brings direct geometry editing and creative modeling into the browser.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Sketch a profile or open a supported model
  2. Extrude and edit the geometry, then inspect the result
  3. Save a project and review the export options for your workflow

Before you rely on it

The public edition is an alpha. Garment fit and manufacturing suitability need independent checks.

Declared input types: model/gltf-binary, model/stl, model/obj, .glb, .stl, .obj, .ply, .usd, .usda

Declared inputs describe expected formats, not certified decoder coverage. Save a separate copy of important work.

How to check 3D model scale before sharing a mesh

A mesh can look correct on screen and arrive at the wrong size in another editor. Use a known dimension and a simple measurement record to distinguish a unit mismatch from a modelling mistake.
