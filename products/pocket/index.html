A GOOD PLACE TO BEGIN

One tool. Three starting points.

1 / 3

A late-night groove

Start with a sparse kick, hat and bass pattern.

  1. Tap a few kick hits, then add hats and a snare or clap.
  2. Add bass notes, choose a scale and experiment with swing.
  3. Balance the voices and export the loop.

A rhythm comparison

Learn how small timing choices change a beat.

  1. Build a simple straight pattern at a comfortable tempo.
  2. Compare different swing amounts without changing the notes.
  3. Export the version whose feel you prefer.

Parts for StemDeck

Carry a sketch into a larger audio session.

  1. Create a drum and bass pattern and check each voice in the mixer.
  2. Download stems as a backup.
  3. Send to StemDeck and confirm the parts arrive before leaving Pocket.

What should I know before starting?

One 16-step bar per pattern; no song mode or pattern chaining. Sounds are synthesized, not sampled kits. Patterns live in local browser storage. Background tabs can drift, and physical-phone audio/download behavior still needs qualification.

How do I keep my work?

Export important ideas. Clearing site data can remove locally saved patterns; browser storage is not an account backup.

Can I download a native app?

This page opens the public web alpha. There are no public native downloads yet. Check current availability.
