A GOOD PLACE TO BEGIN

One tool. Three starting points.

1 / 3

A first artist EPK

Build a concise introduction for a booker or journalist.

  1. Add an artist name, short bio, photo and one representative track.
  2. Include contact information and only quotes you have permission to use.
  3. Preview the layout, download HTML and arrange hosting separately.

A release link page

Give a new release a focused set of links.

  1. Add a short release description and your listening links.
  2. Choose an accent that works with your artwork and review the preview.
  3. Download HTML and test each link before sharing the hosted page.

A printable introduction

Make a compact document for an offline conversation.

  1. Keep the bio concise and use a clearly readable photo.
  2. Check that links and contact details are useful without embedded players.
  3. Print to PDF and review the pages before sending them.

What should I know before starting?

Press does not host your page or provide a messaging backend. Supported music/video services embed; other URLs remain links. Browser printing differs between devices. Local draft storage is not a cloud backup.
