YOUR BROWSER EDITION

Your web edition.

Use Library, Perform, Arrange and Mixer views in StemDeck. Mix decks, arrange audio and MIDI, add built-in effects, capture input and save a portable project.

Current limitations

Arrangement playback is marked as a preview in the current Sattari studio. Browser effects are distinct from native plugin hosting. Track-stem export is not AI stem separation. Save a portable .sattari backup to keep your source audio with your session.

One good first test

  2. 02Explore Perform, then Arrange and the built-in effects.
  3. 03Save a portable .sattari project and export your mix.

SATTARI LABS

Six new tools at sattarimusic.com.

All six are public web alpha, built on the same Sattari site that hosts StemDeck Online.

Split

Separate a song into vocals, drums, bass and other stems on your own device, with mute/solo preview and WAV/ZIP export. The first run downloads a large (172 MB) separation model, which is slow on CPU. Opens straight into StemDeck. Open Split ↗ · More about Split

Key & BPM

Batch key and tempo detection with Camelot notation and CSV export. Half- and double-time results are possible on some tracks. Open Key & BPM ↗ · More about Key & BPM

Vox

Record or drop in a vocal, pitch-correct it, and add a 3rd or 5th harmony; exports a mono dry vocal WAV, which can sound phasey. Open Vox ↗ · More about Vox

Canvas

Audio-reactive looping 9:16 visuals for Spotify Canvas or Reels, exported as MP4 or WebM. Open Canvas ↗ · More about Canvas

Pocket

A mobile-first beat and loop sketchpad; export WAV and stems, or send the sketch straight to StemDeck. Open Pocket ↗ · More about Pocket

Press

Build an artist EPK or link-in-bio page, with HTML and PDF export. Open Press ↗ · More about Press
