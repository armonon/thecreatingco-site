INSIDE GHOST STUDIO

What Ghost Studio can do.

Turn photo references into adjustable 3D garment and object studies.

19features to explore

Explore the tools, workflows and current scope of the product. These are assisted visual approximations. Ghost does not recover exact sewing patterns, measured fit, or unseen construction from photographs.

Give the silhouette room

Look behind the form

YOUR BROWSER EDITION

Your web edition.

Add photos, adjust a garment or object study, and export a GLB or editable project using the real Ghost web app.

Current limitations

Local silhouette and template methods create visual approximations. They do not recover exact patterns or calibrated fit. Recovery belongs to this browser; export a portable backup.
