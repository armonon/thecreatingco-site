Ghost · alpha

Photo-Based 3D Garment Studies

Explore a garment silhouette from front and back photographs. Ghost builds approximate 3D studies for visual experimentation.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Choose clear garment photographs in PNG, JPEG or WebP
  2. Compare the front and back silhouette in the workspace
  3. Review the 3D study and keep the source photographs

Before you rely on it

A photo study does not recover exact sewing patterns, measured fit or hidden construction. Treat it as a visual approximation.

Declared input types: image/png, image/jpeg, image/webp, .ghost, .ghostbatch

Declared inputs describe expected formats, not certified decoder coverage. Save a separate copy of important work.

How to compare front and back garment photographs

A garment study needs consistent views before a 3D approximation is useful. Mismatched pose, distance and cropping can make the same garment look like two different shapes.
