Cutout: Background Removal & Transparent Images

Cutout is the focused image tool for removing a background and refining a transparent image. Open the tool directly, then keep a separate copy of the result you want to use.

Open Cutout ↗

Start with a small project

  1. Choose an image you have permission to edit. Start with a clear subject and a background you can distinguish from it.
  2. Remove the background and inspect fine edges, hair and gaps at a larger scale. Check the result over light and dark backgrounds.
  3. Use the export options currently available in the app. Choose a format that preserves transparency when you need a cutout rather than a flattened image.

Current limits

Public alpha. The catalog lists PNG, JPEG and WebP inputs; this is not a guarantee that every file will decode. Review the exported edges before using the result.

Cutout · alpha

Background Removal & Transparent PNG

Separate a subject from its background and refine the edge. Cutout is a focused image workflow alongside the Luma editor.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Choose a PNG, JPEG or WebP image
  2. Review the subject edge and refine the cutout
  3. Export a transparent PNG and inspect it against a new background

Before you rely on it

Fine hair, translucent objects and difficult edges need review. Automatic background removal is not guaranteed to be perfect.

Declared input types: image/png, image/jpeg, image/webp

Declared inputs describe expected formats, not certified decoder coverage. Save a separate copy of important work.
