A GOOD PLACE TO BEGIN

One tool. Three starting points.

1 / 3

A small merch collection

Compare one graphic on a tee, hoodie and tote.

  1. Open your artwork in Luma, then choose Mockups from the studio menu.
  2. Adjust placement on the tee and compare how it reads on other products.
  3. Export the scenes as a ZIP and inspect them side by side.

An album artwork set

Present a cover as a sleeve and a wall poster.

  1. Use a finished artwork document or upload an image inside Mockups.
  2. Compare the vinyl sleeve and poster scenes; adjust scale separately.
  3. Open a result as a document to add final presentation details.

A colorway comparison

Find a product color that supports the artwork.

  1. Choose one scene and a graphic with a clear silhouette.
  2. Try different product colors and check ink density and contrast.
  3. Download your preferred scene as PNG or JPEG.

What should I know before starting?

These are stylized procedural scenes, not photographs or physically simulated garments. Folds, fabric and glare are approximations. Use them to present a direction, not certify print placement or manufacturing results.

How do I keep my work?

Download the image set and save your original Luma document. Mockup scenes are not editable garment patterns.

Can I download a native app?

This page opens the public web alpha. There are no public native downloads yet. Check current availability.
