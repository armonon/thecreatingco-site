NOW Messenger · Web app alpha

Private messaging.
A little less permanent.

Find your people by username. Talk one-to-one or invite them into a room. Choose when your messages disappear.

Open Circle messages ↗

No email required for a guest identity. Installable as a web app through supported browsers.

Search usernames. Start a DM.

Use your Circle profile or browser guest. In New chat, search at least two characters of a Circle username and select someone to open a direct message. Their Circle messaging preferences apply. You can also share your private contact code.

Invite-only chatrooms

Make a room for your friends or a small group. Send an invitation link, rotate it when needed, and choose a message timer. Up to 50 guests can join a room; only its members can read the conversation.

Messages with an expiry time

Set messages to disappear after 1 minute, 1 hour, 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. A timer change applies to messages sent afterward. Expired messages become unavailable immediately; stored rows are cleaned up on requests. The earlier standalone app also has hourly cleanup.

Guest accounts without email

Circle guests can chat without email or a password. Your name and username come from your Circle profile. Private messaging settings let you clear your NOW messages, memberships and rooms you own while retaining your Circle profile and original inbox.

How private messaging works in NOW

Membership controls who can access a conversation. Username search is available inside the app and excludes expired accounts and blocked contacts. Guest profiles and message history are not public pages for search engines.

NOW does not provide end-to-end encryption. The service operator can access unexpired message content, and recipients can save a copy before it expires. Clearing browser data loses your guest session; there is no email recovery.

Private chats, inside The Circle

NOW lives in Circle’s Messages area, with shared navigation, usernames and profile settings. Start a private DM or room without leaving Circle. The original Circle inbox and earlier standalone NOW chats remain available.

How to check guest chat identity and disappearing messages

A guest chat needs a clear distinction between identity, access and message expiry. Test the current behaviour with harmless text before relying on a room for meaningful conversation.
