What should I know before starting?

Expect bleed and artifacts, especially on dense mixes. The first run downloads a large model and CPU processing can take minutes. Phone/tablet performance is unqualified. Separation does not grant rights to reuse someone else's music.

Can I download a native app?

This page opens the public web alpha. There are no public native downloads yet. Check current availability.

Split · alpha

Audio Stem Separation

Separate a recording into vocals, drums, bass and remaining material for remix exploration. Split runs its model locally after download.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Load audio you have permission to process
  2. Wait for the model and separation, then listen to each part
  3. Download WAV stems or continue toward a StemDeck mix

Before you rely on it

Dense mixes can produce bleed and artifacts. The model download and processing can take time; phone performance is not broadly qualified.

Declared input types: audio/*

Declared inputs describe expected formats, not certified decoder coverage. Save a separate copy of important work.
