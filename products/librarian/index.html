INSIDE LIBRARIAN

What Librarian can do.

Discover books across catalogs, build a personal shelf, and read your PDFs.

16features to explore

Explore the tools, workflows and current scope of the product. Catalogs and AI require available online services. Imported PDFs can be read locally; EPUB links are not a built-in EPUB reader, and catalog records do not guarantee full-text access.

YOUR BROWSER EDITION

Your web edition.

Import your PDFs, read them, build a shelf, and search connected book catalogs.

Current limitations

Catalog search needs a network connection and depends on each source. AI features may send questions and shelf context to the service. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; retain your original files.

Librarian · alpha

Book Discovery & Local PDF Reader

Find reading material and open your own PDFs in Librarian. Keep discovery and a local reading workflow close together.

A practical starting workflow

  1. Browse the available book discovery views
  2. Open a PDF you have permission to read
  3. Review the reader and keep your original document separately

Before you rely on it

Catalog availability and document behavior can vary. Opening a document does not grant rights to redistribute it.

Declared input types: application/pdf, .pdf

Declared inputs describe expected formats, not certified decoder coverage. Save a separate copy of important work.
