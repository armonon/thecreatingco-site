INSIDE LIBRARIAN

What Librarian can do.

Discover books across catalogs, build a personal shelf, and read your PDFs.

16features to explore

Explore the tools, workflows and current scope of the product. Catalogs and AI require available online services. Imported PDFs can be read locally; EPUB links are not a built-in EPUB reader, and catalog records do not guarantee full-text access.

Keep the ideas close.

Open the exhibition brief

After Hours Field Notes and Materials and Sound were imported as original PDFs. The field notes open in a light reading view at 67% zoom.

Follow the sound

Pages five and six connect four measured waveform diagrams with the project handoff. The reader keeps the illustrated spread together.

Change the reading light

The supported Dark control changes the same spread to a dimmer reading view. The page content stays in place for comparison.

Return to the shelf

After a full reload, both PDFs remain in the local library. Materials and Sound reopens with its original diagrams and text.

YOUR BROWSER EDITION

Your web edition.

Import your PDFs, read them, build a shelf, and search connected book catalogs.

Current limitations

Catalog search needs a network connection and depends on each source. AI features may send questions and shelf context to the service. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; retain your original files.
