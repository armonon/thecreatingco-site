Frequently asked questions

How do I convert miles to kilometers?
To convert miles to kilometers, multiply by 1.60934. For example, 5 miles = 5 × 1.60934 = 8.047 km. Select Length in the converter above, set miles as your input unit and kilometers as the output unit, then type your value.
How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?
The formula is: °C = (°F − 32) × 5÷9. For example, 98.6°F = (98.6 − 32) × 5/9 = 37°C. The converter handles this formula automatically — just select Temperature, choose °F as your input and °C as your output.
How many pounds in a kilogram?
There are 2.20462 pounds in 1 kilogram. Conversely, 1 pound = 0.453592 kilograms. To convert kg to lbs, multiply by 2.20462. To convert lbs to kg, multiply by 0.453592.
How many GB in a TB?
There are 1,000 gigabytes (GB) in 1 terabyte (TB) in the decimal (SI) system used by storage manufacturers. In the binary system used by operating systems, 1 tebibyte (TiB) = 1,024 gibibytes (GiB). This converter uses the decimal system.
How do I convert inches to centimeters?
Multiply the number of inches by 2.54. For example, 12 inches = 12 × 2.54 = 30.48 cm. One foot = 30.48 cm and one yard = 91.44 cm.
How do I convert mph to km/h?
Multiply mph by 1.60934 to get km/h. For example, 60 mph = 60 × 1.60934 ≈ 96.56 km/h. To convert km/h to mph, multiply by 0.621371.
How do I convert square feet to square meters?
Multiply by 0.092903. For example, 100 sq ft = 100 × 0.092903 = 9.2903 m². To convert square meters to square feet, multiply by 10.7639.
