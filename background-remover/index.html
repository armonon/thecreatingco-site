Frequently asked questions

Is the background remover really free?
Yes — Cutout is completely free to use in your browser. There is no account, no trial limit, and no download required.
Does the background remover work on portraits?
Yes. Cutout works on portraits, product photos, logos, and any image with a reasonably clear subject. The AI detects the foreground automatically, and you can refine the edge with a brush for hair and complex outlines.
What format does it export?
Cutout exports a transparent PNG — the background pixels become alpha (transparent), so you can place the cutout on any background in an image editor, presentation, or video project.
Does my photo leave my browser?
No — Cutout runs the background removal model locally on your device. Your image is never uploaded to a server.
Can I refine the edge after the automatic removal?
Yes. After the automatic pass, Cutout gives you a grow and shrink brush to add or remove pixels from the mask, so you can clean up hair, fur, or complex edges manually.
Can I use the cutout in a layered editor?
Yes — Cutout integrates with Luma, thecreatingco's layered image editor. You can send your cutout directly into a Luma canvas to composite it with other layers, add effects, or export at full resolution.
What kinds of photos work best?
Cutout works best on photos with clear contrast between the subject and background — portraits, product shots on plain backgrounds, and logos on solid colours. Complex scenes where the subject and background share similar colours may need more manual edge work.
