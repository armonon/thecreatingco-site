Background Remover FAQ

Is the background remover really free?

Yes. Cutout is completely free — no account required, no watermark on the output, and no subscription tier that unlocks features. Open the tool directly in your browser and download your result.

What image formats does it accept?

Cutout accepts PNG, JPEG, and WebP. These cover the vast majority of photos and graphics you would want to cut out. If your file is in a different format, convert it to JPEG or PNG first and then upload it.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

No installation and no account. Cutout runs entirely in your browser. Just open the link, upload your image, and download the result. You can optionally save files to Locker (thecreatingco's in-browser file store) if you want to continue working on a project across sessions.

How do I handle tricky edges — hair, fur, or glass?

After the initial background removal, use the edge inspection view to zoom in and check problem areas. Toggle between a light and a dark preview background to catch halos and semi-transparent fringing before you export. Fine hair on a plain background usually cuts cleanly; complex scenes with overlapping colour may need manual refinement.

What does the output file look like?

Cutout exports a transparent PNG with a full alpha channel. The subject keeps its original colours; the background area becomes fully transparent. You can drop the result straight into Figma, Luma, Photoshop, or any design tool that accepts transparent images.

Can I use the cutout in other thecreatingco tools?

Yes. Export the transparent PNG from Cutout and open it in Luma to continue editing as a layered image, or bring the subject into Mockups to place it onto a product shot. Files saved to Locker are available across all thecreatingco apps without re-uploading.
