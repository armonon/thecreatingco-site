# Year One — execution control

Plan window: 2026-10-09 through 2027-10-09. Owner direction received 2026-10-09.
This is a strategy/target document; the SQLite task queue remains the work authority.

## North star

Build a focused creative-technology company around four dependable flagships
(ScenePilot, Luma, Form and StemDeck), a validated free-tool acquisition layer,
one evidence-backed project utility, a real cross-app workflow, ethical
user-led distribution and a validated revenue path. The 9/10 quality and
$10k–$100k monthly revenue trajectory are stretch targets, not forecasts. Actual
company health and product quality scores stay unknown until a rubric and direct
evidence support scoring. No fifth flagship or new major app without validated
exceptional demand and strategic value.

## Portfolio allocation (planning targets)

- 45% — flagship products and reliability
- 20% — free web-tool acquisition platform (FileLab is still held at validation)
- 15% — distribution, marketing and SEO
- 10% — ProjectLab / cross-app foundations (Project Rescue is still held at validation)
- 10% — revenue experiments

These are not observed labor percentages. P0/P1 security, data-loss, crash or
core-workflow risks may temporarily override the target. Review allocation
monthly; existing release approvals and product holds remain intact.

## Engine plan and stage gates

**Product:** finish and measure each flagship golden workflow; preserve real user
files; no new features ahead of reliability/onboarding/export gaps. Real-world
projects and consent-based feedback become evidence, not quality claims.

**Distribution:** keep a useful, coherent tools directory; make first-use,
availability, SEO, performance and analytics trustworthy. First correct known
availability/count contradictions. No paid/organic scale-up until funnel data and
product onboarding exist. Campaigns follow the existing marketing approvals.

**Revenue:** preserve the free-core promise. Fastest prepared low-risk validation
is Glyph’s first-font/return-value E02/W03 journey (five consent-compatible creator
journeys, no new spend, six-hour preparation cap, up to seven days); it is
PREPARED_NOT_LAUNCHED and not evidence of willingness to pay. Do not contact people
or publish pricing without applicable approval. Consider Stripe/Sattari sales only
after company attribution, fulfillment, margin and capacity evidence.

**ProjectLab / CreativeOS:** first evaluate Locker against a synthetic portability
and recovery need; test exact file round trips. Validate demand before a Project
Rescue prototype. One demonstrated portable handoff is not live sync. Build one
actual cross-app edit/save/reopen workflow before claiming a CreativeOS foundation.

## Milestones

- **Q1 / control and quality (Oct 9, 2026–Jan 8, 2027):** reconcile public
  availability/catalog; establish analytics/financial access or preserve explicit
  blockers; resolve highest-severity release/workflow defects; choose real golden
  fixtures; run consent-based beta validation only after approvals. No new app by
  default.
- **Q2 / distribution and launch (Jan 9–Apr 8, 2027):** release the first
  qualified flagship; build FileLab only if validation explicitly CONTINUES;
  prepare evidence-led tutorials and a real four-product workflow demo. Verify
  analytics and activation measurement before acquisition experiments.
- **Q3 / monetization and differentiation (Apr 9–Jul 8, 2027):** test a paid offer
  only after demonstrated product value, delivery readiness, real willingness-to-pay
  evidence and approvals. Validate Project Rescue before prototyping; prototype one
  Open In/live-link workflow if the shared asset contract is sound.
- **Q4 / focus and scale (Jul 9–Oct 9, 2027):** reallocate to products/channels
  with measured adoption and retention; stop/pause only through existing owner
  approval; demonstrate one real cross-app creative workflow and update Year Two
  from actual results.

Targets are revisited monthly. If evidence is absent, report the milestone as
unverified, not achieved.
