## Small observed workflow

Run only with a consenting participant, after recruitment gates clear. The steps below describe proposed participant actions; they have not been completed as human observations.

1. Confirm the participant’s recent real lettering need, intended destination app/device/version, and that the chosen artwork is theirs or they have permission to use it. Invite them to choose a short sample made from supported characters; don’t provide a canned font/sample as if they made it.
2. In the public Glyph alpha, draw the chosen characters and inspect spacing/preview. Observe unaided behavior first; note each intervention separately. Stop if the output is unusable or data is at risk.
3. Save a portable project backup. Close and reopen **the test project only**, then confirm it restores. Never close unrelated work that may be unsaved.
4. Export TTF for the kerning path. (OTF export is available, but current evidence says it does not preserve kerning.) Record export errors, missing glyphs/fallbacks, and spacing issues without framing them as user error.
5. In the participant’s own destination app, install/select or otherwise make the TTF available using that app’s ordinary workflow; enter the sample and save the destination document. Close/reopen that document and check whether the text remains usable. Do not presume the app supports this; document the exact app/version and outcome. Avoid changing system font settings without the participant’s informed agreement.
6. Ask feedback only after they have attempted the workflow. Do not ask for private documents, recordings, or artwork copies. Recording/media requires separate appropriate consent; otherwise take minimal deidentified notes.

## Five feedback questions

1. What was the last real job where you needed custom lettering or a font, and what did you need to deliver?
2. At which step, if any, did you hesitate, need help, hit an error, or get an unexpected result? What did you expect instead?
3. Could you save and reopen the Glyph project and export a TTF with the characters and spacing you wanted? What was missing or hard to judge?
4. Could you use that TTF in your chosen app and reopen the destination document? What exact app/device/version did you use, and what happened (including failure or fallback)?
5. For that real job, would this workflow replace any part of your current method? What would you still do elsewhere, and what workaround/time/cost—if any—does that currently involve?
