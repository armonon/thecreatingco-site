## Million-dollar scenario sanity checks

These are arithmetic, not forecasts or assigned probabilities; prices and repeat purchase behavior are hypotheses unless labeled current.

- **Primary, narrow creator workflow plus qualified product:** at hypothetical $499 one-time average ticket, need **2,005 sales/year** to exceed $1M; at $199 annual paid product, **5,026 annual customers**. Manual $499 service alone implies ~167 jobs/month, so measure labor/capacity before extrapolating. Service can validate/finance the workflow; product must earn repeatable demand to scale.
- **Secondary, studio/education implementation:** hypothetical $5,000 annual contract needs **200 customers**; $10,000 needs **100**. Existing lessons are raw material, not a deployed district-ready product. Pilot $2,500 workshop/setup tests buyer/scope; do not claim recurring revenue for a one-time workshop. Procurement and support make 48-hour sales unrealistic; 48-hour signal is a qualified decision-maker conversation or scoped pilot request.
- **Experimental, specialized creative product:** hypothetical $99 one-time license needs **10,102 sales/year**, or $199 annual plan needs **5,026 paid annual customers**. Test one workflow/segment, not all 44 registry entries. Distribution and quality unproven. Existing Lite promise preserved.
- **Bridge, local physical business:** current $25 room-hour cannot reach $1M in one six-hours/night room; gear gross may be scalable but inventory/COGS/cash requirements unknown. Do not book Sattari gross as thecreateco revenue without verified attribution.
