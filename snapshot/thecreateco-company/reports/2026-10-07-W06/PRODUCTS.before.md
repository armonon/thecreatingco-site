**Cast** (Form Studio workspace) — Print/cast prep for jewelry: ring size lookup (US/UK/EU/JP) and bore-fit detection on a model, wall/prong thickness check (inward ray-cast, red=below minimum wall, amber=below minimum prong, with material presets labeled "guidance values only"), mesh health (open edges, non-manifold edges, inverted faces, loose shells) with one-click basic repair (undoable), resin-print orientation suggestions (support angle, overhangs, islands, top-3 ranked), STL export. The codebase has a unit test that fails if the words "certified," "guaranteed," or "ready to manufacture" ever appear in Cast's UI — treat that as the hard line for copy. **Demo hook:** load a ring STL, run Thickness check — a 0.6 mm prong lights up red/amber instantly; or Suggest orientations on a pendant and watch the top-3 tilt angles.

**Mockup** (Luma Studio, "…" menu → Mockups) — puts your Luma document or an uploaded image onto 6 procedurally rendered scenes (T-shirt, hoodie, tote, framed poster, vinyl sleeve, phone screen) with perspective-correct placement, fold/seam displacement and shading, and overlay effects (drawstrings, tote handle, glass/screen glare). PR armonon/luma-studio#1, **merged 2026-10-06**, LIVE at https://luma.thecreatingco.com/studio. **Honest limits:** "stylised procedural renders, not photos" (PR's own words) — reads as clean product illustration, not studio photography; folds are placed by hand, not simulated; source art capped at 2048 px; every scene image is generated at runtime, no stock photography used. **Demo hook:** drop one piece of album art in, export the 6-scene ZIP, show tee/hoodie/tote/poster/vinyl/phone side by side.

**AutoCut** (ScenePilot, dedicated app) — drop a song plus many clips (or a folder); reads tempo/beats/downbeats/bars/energy/drop, guesses song sections, skips bad clips (black/blown-out/blank/out-of-focus), cuts on the beat with density following energy, gives every section start and the drop a fresh "hero" shot, never repeats a clip back-to-back. Presets: Music video / Hype edit / Chill-lyric / Trailer, plus a density slider and seeded re-roll. **Performance mode** cross-correlates multiple takes against the song so cuts between takes stay lip-synced (verified in the PR's own test: takes aligned to within 10 ms, synced shots landed within 0–1 frame of plan). PRs armonon/scenepilot-studio-badass#86 (stacks on #85) and #87 (production polish) — both **open/draft in the repo**, but the feature is published and LIVE at https://scenepilot.thecreatingco.com/tools/autocut/ (confirmed today: page title "AutoCut: Free Auto Music Video Editor That Cuts to the Beat | ScenePilot" matches the PR's indexing work exactly). No dedicated `autocut.thecreatingco.com` domain yet — that's a later DNS step per PR #87, not done. **Critical honest limit — repeat this every time AutoCut is claimed to work on real footage: all of its testing so far (the 805/805 unit tests, the headless-Chrome end-to-end pass, the lip-sync/alignment numbers) ran on synthetic, ffmpeg-generated media (a generated 120 BPM tone-click song, generated B-roll, generated "performance takes" with added echo/noise) — not on real phone recordings.** Other limits: beats/bars come from a classical tracker plus a small downbeat model, so half/double-time and a wrong "1" are possible; sections are guessed from loudness/texture only, no lyric or harmony analysis; clip "quality" judgment is purely technical (exposure, detail, motion), not content-aware; hard cuts only, transitions/effects/titles are added afterward in the ScenePilot editor; Safari and Firefox export paths are unverified. **Demo hook:** drop one song + 3–6 clips, hit a preset, watch the timeline fill with cuts landing on the beat in seconds.

**Board** (ScenePilot) — paste a screenplay (Fountain-ish headings/character cues/parentheticals/transitions) or plain prose/bullets, get auto-generated scenes and shots with a guessed shot type, lens, and duration; shots can be edited, sketched on (pen/eraser/undo, pressure-sensitive), given an uploaded reference frame, reordered, duplicated, or deleted; 4 aspect ratios; autosaves to IndexedDB; exports Print-to-PDF, CSV, or board JSON, and imports into ScenePilot as scene/shot markers plus a shot-list brief. **Grade** (ScenePilot) — pick a reference still, get a CIELAB color-transfer onto your clip or image (Balanced/Reinhard/Histogram methods, video measured on 8 sampled frames), WebGL2 3D-LUT preview with a before/after split on live video, intensity and palette controls, `.cube` export (17/33/65 size) and a graded PNG still. **Reframe** (ScenePilot) — drop a horizontal video, pick 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1, and get an automatic subject-following crop: detects faces (reuses AutoCut's self-hosted BlazeFace) with a motion-saliency fallback when no face is found, builds a smooth virtual camera (dead zone, max pan speed, easing) that resets cleanly on a detected scene cut instead of whip-panning, keeps two faces in frame together when they fit or follows the larger/active one, side-by-side preview with the crop window drawn on the source, a timeline with draggable keyframes to override the automatic path, and a per-segment "letterbox instead" option. Exports MP4/WebM (WebCodecs, with a real-time MediaRecorder fallback) with audio, plus the crop path as JSON; cheap "Open in AutoCut"/"Open in editor" links. PR armonon/scenepilot-studio-badass#88 (stacks on #87), **open/draft in the repo**, but LIVE at https://scenepilot.thecreatingco.com/tools/reframe/ via an additive overlay publish (same method as AutoCut/Captions/Board/Grade — see CATALOG.md/WAVE2.md). **Honest limits:** subject tracking is per-frame re-detection, not real tracking — a fast-moving, turned-away, or small/backlit subject can be lost, and the camera then follows whatever has the most motion; multi-subject mode only ever considers the two largest faces, a third subject can be left out of frame; scene-cut detection is tuned for hard cuts, not pans/dissolves/flashes; the camera only pans and recenters, it never zooms; the MediaRecorder export fallback is WebM-only and runs in real time with the tab visible; all testing so far is on short, ffmpeg-generated synthetic clips (a moving bright square, an AI-generated test-photo face), not a broad range of real footage.

**Split** (Sattari) — drop one song (WAV/MP3/FLAC/M4A/OGG, up to 10 minutes / 100 MB) and get 4 stems (vocals, drums, bass, instruments) via HTDemucs (Meta, MIT license) running on-device via ONNX, fetched from Hugging Face at a pinned, SHA-256-checked revision; mute/solo each stem, download WAV, or send straight into StemDeck. PR armonon/sattari-custom-site#23 (open in the repo, **published to production via an additive overlay deploy, not a git merge** — see CATALOG.md), confirmed LIVE today at https://sattarimusic.com/studio/split with the full page copy fetched directly. **Honest limits, quoted verbatim from the live page — this is the Split quality/speed caveat the task asked for:** "Quality is HTDemucs-class, not studio grade: expect some bleed between stems and artifacts on dense mixes." **Vox** (Sattari) — record or drop a solo vocal (up to 3 minutes), YIN-based pitch detection finds the key, a PSOLA pitch shifter pulls every note to the scale at adjustable strength, adds a diatonic 3rd or 5th harmony generated from the same take, exports WAV. LIVE at https://sattarimusic.com/studio/vox, same PR #23. **Honest limits:** needs one clean, solo (monophonic) voice — backing tracks, reverb, or polyphonic audio confuse the pitch tracker; no neural pitch model, so "large corrections and harmonies can sound phasey or robotic" (site's words); harmony is a single voice that moves exactly with the lead, not an independent performance; processing is offline/batch (re-renders in about a second), not live correction while singing.

**Canvas** (Sattari) — drop a track and optional cover art, get a looping audio-reactive 9:16 video for Spotify Canvas, Reels, and TikTok (scene/palette controls, kick-reactive intensity, title/artist text overlay), export MP4 (where the browser supports it) or WebM, optionally with audio baked in for Reels/TikTok (Spotify Canvas itself must be silent). **Press** (Sattari) — build an artist EPK / link-in-bio page (name, tagline, location, photo, bio, links, tracks, press quotes, contact info, accent color/layout), download it as a single HTML file to self-host anywhere (your own site, Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages), or print it to PDF via the browser.
