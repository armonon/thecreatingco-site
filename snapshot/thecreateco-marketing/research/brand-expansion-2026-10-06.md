# Brand expansion + new apps — 2026-10-06 (Armon, repeatedly: "keep going and brainstorm new apps and ways to expand our brand")

Position to own: **the independent creative toolkit — free to start, works in your browser, your files stay yours.** Everything below should reinforce that (no lock-in, no subscription pressure, honest alpha).

## A. Make the product feel like one brand (not 23 links)
1. **Installable + offline (PWA)** across apps — pilot on Glyph Studio (own site, low risk; service workers on big live sites need care), then roll out. Delivers "creativity shouldn't have an off switch".
2. **Locker** — one local library + "send to" between apps (Pocket → StemDeck → AutoCut, Luma → Mockup/Canvas, Glyph font → Luma/Lyric). Kickstarter priority #3.
3. **One design language** — shared header/launcher/footer, one name per app (fix StemDeck's four names), app subdomains everywhere (glyph.*, autocut.*).

## B. Community & growth loops
4. **Make a Thing Monday** — weekly creative prompt using one tool (from the "Make a thing. Make some noise. Make it yours." line); entries posted to the Community board; best ones featured on the site + @armon3.
5. **Made with thecreateco** showcase — opt-in, reviewed gallery fed by an in-app "share" button; doubles as Kickstarter proof.
6. **Remix the Orbit** — open contest using the Orbit source bundle (pendant → poster → soundtrack → film).
7. **Founding Creators** — first 25 creators who give real feedback get credited on the site and early access (no paid promises without Armon).

## C. Free stuff that spreads (lead magnets, SEO)
8. **Free packs** at thecreatingco.com/packs — LUT pack (made with Grade), Canvas visual presets, Pocket drum-kit patterns, a font drawn in Glyph Studio, Luma brushes. Real files, no email wall (launch list offered, not required).
9. **thecreateco Type** — a small free-font foundry: fonts drawn in Glyph Studio, released free. Font sites are huge discovery channels.
10. **Toolbox** — tiny free utilities people google daily (tuner, metronome, tap BPM, ring-size chart, aspect ratio, palette from image, LUFS meter).
11. **Open-source engines** — publish small libraries (Glyph curve fitter, beat/energy analysis, LUFS meter) → developer goodwill, GitHub stars, backlinks.

## D. Education
12. **thecreateco School** — the 43 lessons + new-tool "try this first" steps as free short courses; 60-second "one tool, one result" videos.
13. **Classroom mode** for design/architecture/fashion/music programs (no accounts, works on school Chromebooks) — students become lifelong users.

## E. Reach & distribution
14. **One launch per tool** — Product Hunt / Show HN (Armon writes HN posts personally — HN bans AI-written posts) / relevant newsletters, spaced weekly instead of one big bang.
15. **Native releases** — ship the notarized Mac candidates (Luma, Ghost, Librarian, Form) as "public beta" downloads once licensing gates clear; Chrome Web Store listings for PWAs.
16. **Integrations, not replacements** — export to Blender/Premiere/Ableton/Figma formats; ScenePilot Premiere panel exists in scenepilot-compositor. Be the friendly companion to the big tools.
17. **Localization** — Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese for landing pages first.

## F. Physical & story
18. **The Orbit pendant, for real** — design in Form, check in Cast, cast in silver: a real object made entirely in our tools. Story content + natural Kickstarter reward.
19. **Merch made with Mockup** — print-on-demand drop designed in Luma/Glyph (spend = Armon's call).
20. **Artbook / zine** of community work for the Kickstarter.

## G. New apps (beyond wave 3's Lyric, Clean, Reframe)
- **Animate** — 2D frame-by-frame + tween animation (biggest gap in the suite).
- **Portfolio** — Press for visual artists: one-page portfolio from your Luma/Form/ScenePilot exports.
- **Live** — Canvas as a live VJ tool reacting to a mic/line-in for performers.
- **Brand Kit** — logo, palette and type system from Glyph + Luma; exports a brand sheet.
- **Moodboard** — collect references from anywhere, then send to Luma/Form/Board.
- **Chop**, **Chords**, **Thumb**, **Cutout/Upscale**, **Script**, **Pattern**, **Room** (see app-ideas-wave3).
