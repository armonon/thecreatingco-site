Open StemDeck

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open StemDeck in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. You may need More to find Share.
  3. Leave Open as Web App enabled if shown, then tap Add.

Launch from the new icon. Use landscape on iPhone for more timeline space; iPad works best for full arrangements.

This installs the web edition, not an App Store binary. A native iPhone/iPad beta is being prepared; no public TestFlight build is available yet.

Mac

In Safari on macOS Sonoma or later, open StemDeck, then choose File → Add to Dock.

A separate downloadable Mac web-engine app is in developer testing. Its public installer will be listed here after notarization and validation.

Before you record

StemDeck works offline after the first visit: open it once with internet and it keeps opening without a connection (AI stem separation needs its model saved for offline in Split first). Keep the app in the foreground while performing or recording; screen locking or switching apps can interrupt audio on mobile. No background-recording guarantee is made.

Allow microphone access only when you need an input. Start with a short test recording and export it. MIDI, output-device selection, plugin hosting and separation capabilities depend on the platform—this edition does not add native AU/VST hosting.

Projects are stored locally in each app/browser, not automatically synced across devices. Export your project and recordings before switching devices, clearing website data or removing the app.
