Scope: implement the five priorities from the UI review, preserving advanced editing capabilities. These changes are local; no deployment, commit, or signed installer was requested in this pass.

## Implemented

- Auto Cut now follows Media → Timing & style → Generate → Review & apply. Timing policy precedes generation; draft selection and Apply follow it. Lighting, lyrics, and specialist controls live in an optional-refinements disclosure.
- Isolated draft-cut playback and scrubbing use the shared compositor and the draft's canonical video clips without replacing the live timeline. This is explicitly a **silent cut review**: clip effects, compositions, transitions and titles are shown; the full audio mix and global look are reviewed after Apply. Undo restores the previous edit.
- Delivery now mounts the real main-sequence preview, with playback/scrubbing, an editable output name and save-location guidance. Temporary effect auditions and color bypass cannot change the delivery preview. Output details, custom settings, diagnostics and additional delivery types remain available in disclosures. The queue appears for existing/active jobs, including paused queues; cancellation remains visible.
- Navigation has stable Tools and Simple/Advanced labels. Transcript replaces the ambiguous Text tab label. Composition tabs support opening, closing, pinning and renaming (double-click or F2), plus per-project saved tab preferences. Closing a tab does not delete its composition. New documents start without sample compositions; Project Media offers samples explicitly.
- Effect browsing clearly distinguishes sample scenes, unapplied previews, and applied effects, names the target, and does not offer locked clips as audition targets. Immediate drag-to-apply behavior is documented in the browser.
- Shared controls, fields and status text received larger typography, stronger focus indicators and more generous target sizes. Advanced capabilities remain reachable rather than being removed.
