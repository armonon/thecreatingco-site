A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
press play.

Is this only for music videos?

No. Music-aware editing is a strength, but the timeline, compositions, color and audio workflows also support narrative edits, social videos and motion-led visual work. Music and Cinematic modes are creative starting points, not separate project formats.

Is the automation editable?

Yes. Auto Director creates an editable arrangement. You can change the pacing, adjust timing, protect choices and refine individual clips. Analysis is a starting point; it is not a guarantee of perfect transient detection or creative judgment.

Are these real product screenshots?

The workspace tour uses captures from the current ScenePilot development build: an imported image in the master sequence, a built-in motion composition and the FX browser. The Look Lab and pacing demo are clearly labeled website illustrations. Public release builds may lag behind the development build.

Does my footage need to go to the cloud?

No source-media upload is required for the core local editing workflow. Browser Lite uses your browser; Desktop Pro adds native local media tooling. Local AI features require their supported models and hardware.

Is ScenePilot a complete Adobe replacement?

Not yet. ScenePilot brings editing, music automation and motion into one developing suite. It is an early-access product, not a claim of full Adobe feature, plugin or production-certification parity. Test important workflows with your own media before relying on it for a delivery.
