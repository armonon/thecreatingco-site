Frequently asked questions

Is the vocal remover really free?
Yes — Split is completely free. No account, no download, and no trial limit. Upload your song and the vocal separation starts immediately.
How does vocal removal work?
Split uses an AI stem separation model to isolate the vocal track from the rest of the audio. Once separated, you can mute the vocals for an instrumental or solo them for an acapella.
Can I get the acapella (vocals only) as well?
Yes. Split separates the full mix into vocals, drums, bass and melody. Solo the vocal stem to hear just the acapella, then download it as an audio file.
Does my audio leave my browser?
No — Split's model runs locally after the initial model load. Your audio is never uploaded to a server.
What audio formats does it support?
Split accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG and most other formats your browser can decode.
Will the vocal removal be perfect?
AI stem separation works well on most professionally recorded music. Heavily compressed or live recordings, or songs where the vocals and instruments share similar frequencies, may have some bleed. Complex mixes may benefit from some cleanup in a DAW after export.
Can I use the stems in my own music or video?
Split gives you downloadable stem files to use in a DAW or video editor. Whether the original track's copyright permits this is a separate question — always make sure you have permission to use the source audio in your project.
