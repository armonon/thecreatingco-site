Website & analytics

The main marketing hub keeps creative projects locally; its Community offers email-code sign-in and hosted public discussions, described below. We use Netlify Web Analytics to understand pageviews, unique visitors, referring sources and popular pages. Analytics is processed on Netlify’s servers from website requests; we have not added a browser analytics script or analytics cookies. Visitor counts estimate website traffic, not verified accounts or active product users.

Netlify hosts the main site and processes ordinary request information, including IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect it. Visiting a linked application brings you to that application's hosting and data practices.

02

Projects on this device

The learning workspaces save drafts and campaign files in this browser using local storage or IndexedDB. Bounce can save selected audio, queue order and EQ when you choose Save library here. Reclaim's browser companion examines selected files for size and duplicate comparison without changing the originals. These companions do not upload selected files for those local operations.

Browser storage is specific to the website address, browser and device. It is not an account backup and may be cleared or evicted. Keep original files and portable exports. Browser site-data controls remove local copies. Bounce also offers Clear browser library; that does not delete your original files. The older thecreatingco player remains available to export libraries saved on this address before moving to Bounce's main site.

My Projects

The My Projects workspace stores projects, notes and file versions in IndexedDB on this browser and device. It does not automatically capture changes made in an app or sync your projects to an account.

ZIP backup and restore use files you explicitly export or import. Moving work between apps requires downloading and importing it. Keep an exported backup outside your browser; archiving a project retains its local files and can be reversed.

Directory favorites are stored in local storage in this browser. They are not synced to an account. Unsave individual destinations in the directory, or clear this website’s browser data to remove the saved list.

03

Support & submissions

Support and feedback

Choosing Send message shares your request type, product, message, any optional email address and device details, and a report reference with thecreatingco through Netlify Forms. Netlify also processes submission and request information for delivery and spam filtering. Messages are accessible to authorized administrators of the site's hosting account, not displayed publicly.

We use those messages to investigate problems, improve products, answer requests and prevent abuse. An email address is optional, but we cannot reply without one. This form does not accept attachments. Download a copy creates a file on your device and does not submit it.

Messages remain in the support inbox until an administrator deletes them; automatic expiration is not currently configured. To request removal, use the privacy request option and identify the original message. For Netlify's own processing and retention, read Netlify's privacy policy.

Remix submissions

Submitting a remix sends your nickname, optional email, public result and project links, credits, remix permission and description to the same private thecreatingco support inbox through Netlify. Approved submissions may be published with your nickname and credits; your email is not part of the public gallery. Your nickname is remembered locally in this browser for convenience. Use Support to request removal.

Saved lessons in Your work stay in browser storage; these are not uploaded by the save action.

thecreatingco launch list

Joining the launch list sends your email address and explicit consent to thecreatingco through Netlify Forms, using our existing private intake inbox and the reference founding-list-v1. We use it to contact you about the Kickstarter announcement, any Founding Edition details, new builds and major product updates. Joining does not create an account, make a pledge or reserve a reward.

Signup requests are retained until an administrator removes them; automatic expiration and self-service unsubscribe are not configured. To withdraw consent or request deletion, use the privacy contact form with the email you signed up with. We will handle removal manually. The form does not publish your email.

04

The Circle

The Circle includes public profiles, posts, collections, discussions, direct messages, shared listening rooms and Pocket Studio recordings.

Accounts, sign-in and guest posts

Updated October 5, 2026. Community sign-in uses Supabase to verify your email and manage sign-in sessions. Resend delivers sign-in emails. Your email is not shown in public Community feeds.

Guest threads and replies do not require sign-in. A private HttpOnly cookie, lasting up to 30 days, identifies the browser that owns your guest posts. Clearing it removes your ability to manage those posts from that browser. Images require verified NOW sign-in. JPEG, PNG and WebP images are resized and converted to JPEG in your browser before upload, discarding source metadata.

Published names, threads, replies and image previews are public. Private drafts and unpublished attachments are not public. Community content, moderation records and rate-limit counters are stored in Cloudflare D1 and R2 through our hosted service. Supabase, Resend and hosting providers process information needed to operate these services.

Posting and sign-in abuse controls use counters keyed by daily hashes of network and email addresses, rather than storing raw addresses in those counters. Session cookies are HttpOnly. Bookmarks and saved creative projects may be stored on your device. Public names are handles, not verified legal identities.

Authors can withdraw threads; this hides content from feeds but does not guarantee immediate deletion of stored records or provider backups. Moderators can review reports, hide content and suspend posting. An automatic retention/deletion schedule is not yet established. Records remain until removed through an administrative request or service maintenance. Do not post sensitive personal information.

For privacy or deletion requests, use our support page and identify the affected post or account. We may verify ownership before acting. Provider policies: Supabase, Resend, Cloudflare and OpenAI hosting.

Profiles, links and published work

Public profile names, handles, bios, links, collections and published posts can be viewed by other visitors. Added links may include a title, description, preview image and original publication date. Following, reactions, bookmarks and notification records support your network activity. A saved external link is not a preserved copy of the original post or website. Opening external links or playing third-party embeds contacts the linked service, which has its own data practices.

Direct messages

Circle’s original inbox and NOW private chats use a verified Circle account or browser guest. Message text, sender, conversation membership, timestamps and read markers are stored by our hosted service in Cloudflare D1. The application restricts reading to conversation members, subject to account and blocking rules. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted; authorized service administrators and hosting providers can access stored data when operating or investigating the service. Recipients can keep copies. Do not send credentials or sensitive personal information.

Uploaded images, audio and video

Uploading media sends the selected file or browser-prepared copy to our hosted service. File objects are stored in Cloudflare R2, and related records such as owner, title, credits, type, size, processing status, duration and waveform are stored in D1. Published media is available with its post; unpublished uploads are not intended as a public gallery. Images are optimized copies, not untouched originals. Do not assume audio or video metadata is removed.

Upload only material you have permission to share.

Pocket Studio recordings

Recording requires your browser's microphone permission. Recordings and their names are kept in IndexedDB on this browser and device until you remove them or browser storage is cleared or evicted. Recording and trimming do not by themselves publish the audio. Publishing requires verified NOW sign-in and uploads the selected audio publicly to The Circle, with an option to include it in Songs. Removing a local recording does not remove a published copy.

Download originals or trimmed WAV files to keep a backup outside the browser.

Sessions and listening-room chat

Our service stores room titles, hosts, visibility settings, track queues, playback position and timing, room controls and chat messages in D1. Participants receive this state to keep playback aligned. Public rooms can be discovered in Sessions. Unlisted rooms are omitted from public discovery, but anyone with the room link may access them unless blocked or removed; they are not private, invite-only conversations. Queued audio comes from shared tracks, not a live microphone broadcast.

Chat names, messages and timestamps are visible to people who can access the room.

Removal, retention and copies

Withdrawing a post or removing a comment hides it through the application; hiding content is not a guarantee that underlying database records, uploaded objects or backups are immediately erased. Closing a listening room ends its active controls but does not erase its stored chat. There is no general automatic deletion schedule for messages, room history or published media. Browser-storage removal affects local copies only.

Other users may already have downloaded or copied public content or messages.

For account, direct-message, recording, room-history or uploaded-file deletion requests, use the privacy support form. Identify the account and relevant post, conversation or room; ownership verification may be required. Do not include the private message or recording itself unless it is necessary to explain the request. Requests are handled manually, including checking related storage and provider limitations.

NOW Messenger

NOW Messenger is a separate app for guest direct messages and invite-only chatrooms. Its guest identities and conversations are not imported from The Circle or NOW sign-in.

Guest identities and discovery

You can create a guest without an email or password. The app stores a server-verified guest session in a browser cookie for up to 30 days. Clearing browser data loses access; there is no email recovery.

In Circle, username search finds Circle profiles and messaging is controlled by their Circle preferences and blocks. Contact codes and links can also be shared. The earlier standalone app retains optional guest usernames and its separate discovery settings. Display names are not verified identities.

Messages, access and expiry

Guest and conversation records, membership and message content are stored in the app’s dedicated Cloudflare D1 database. Conversation membership restricts reading. Anyone who possesses a room invitation can use it to join; share invitation and contact links carefully.

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Service operators with database access can read stored, unexpired messages. Recipients can copy or capture content before it disappears.

Each new message receives the conversation’s selected expiry timer. Changing the timer applies to future messages. Expired messages are excluded from app reads immediately. Physical database cleanup runs on requests. The earlier standalone app also has an hourly cleanup task; on an idle site, expired rows may remain until the next successful cleanup.

Deleting a guest

Circle’s private messaging settings can delete your NOW messages, memberships and rooms you own while preserving your Circle profile and original inbox. Earlier standalone Guest settings can delete that app’s guest identity and its authored messages, memberships and owned conversations. This does not erase recipients’ copies, screenshots or provider backups.

Read the NOW Messenger guide for its guest, discovery and message-timer controls. Access to the hosted app and membership of a conversation are separate controls.

Choices & privacy requests

Use thecreatingco support for privacy questions or deletion requests. Include the product, your message reference if you have one, and an email address for a reply. Do not send credentials or identity documents. We may need to confirm ownership before acting on a request.

Help us find the right record.

  • Name the product or service.
  • Include a post, conversation, room or message reference if you have one.
  • Provide an email address if you would like a reply.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes or identity documents. For private messages and recordings, describe the request without including the content unless it is necessary.

Removal is different in each place

Clearing browser storage removes local copies. It does not remove published uploads, support submissions or data held by a linked service. The relevant sections above explain the available controls and their limits.

Notice updates and alpha testing

We will update this notice when the website's data handling changes. The Circle and NOW Messenger are described in this notice. Linked collaboration workspaces and product applications may have additional notices. During alpha, work on copies and keep an export outside your browser.
