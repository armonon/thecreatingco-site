A FEW STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you join.

Is the Kickstarter live?

We’re preparing for Kickstarter. A public campaign link and launch date have not been announced here. Join the list for the announcement; this website is not collecting pledges.

Can I use the software now?

Yes. Our public tools are available to explore, including four Creative Suite products. Editions differ and many features are still alpha. Check each product page and the release-status page for current capabilities.

What would funding support?

Our proposed priorities are reliability, native app qualification, better file handoffs, wider platform access, documentation and creator feedback. Specific commitments and budget allocations will be published with the campaign.

Will there be a subscription or a Founding Edition?

Pricing, licensing, reward terms and any Founding Edition offer have not been announced. Signing up does not reserve a price or entitlement.

Does everything work offline or on Windows?

Windows and Mac Pro editions are in development; mobile editions are planned. There are no public native downloads yet. Offline capabilities and supported devices will be confirmed for each release.

What does joining the list do?

It sends your email and consent privately to thecreatingco so we can contact you about the launch and major product updates. It is not a payment or an account. Use the privacy contact form to request removal.

How can I help today?

Try a real project and tell us what worked, what failed and what you were trying to make. Specific feedback helps us choose the next fixes.
