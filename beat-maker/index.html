Frequently asked questions

Is Pocket really free?
Yes — Pocket is completely free to use in your browser. No account, no credit card, no download required. Just open it and start making beats.
What instruments does the beat maker include?
Pocket includes synthesized drums (kick, snare, hi-hat, open hi-hat) and a bass synthesizer with pitch-per-step sequencing. Each lane has 16 steps per bar. You can set the key and scale for the bass synth to keep everything in tune.
Can I export my beats as audio files?
Yes. Once your pattern is done, you can export each lane as a separate WAV stem or export the full mix. Stems can also be sent to StemDeck for further arrangement.
Does Pocket work on mobile?
Pocket runs in any modern web browser on desktop or mobile. The 16-step grid is touch-friendly and no installation is needed.
How do I set the tempo and swing?
Use the BPM control to set your tempo (40–200 BPM) and the swing knob to add rhythmic feel. Changes apply live while the loop plays — you can adjust while listening.
Can I use my Pocket beat in other apps?
Yes. Export WAV stems and import them into any DAW (Ableton, Logic, FL Studio, etc.), or use the StemDeck handoff to continue building your track in a full arrangement environment within thecreatingco ecosystem.
