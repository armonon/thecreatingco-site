Beat elements reference

Classic patterns and what each drum element does in a mix.

Element Role in the mix Classic placement Genre examples
Kick drum Low-end foundation, drives the groove Beats 1 & 3 (4/4) Hip-hop, house, trap, EDM
Snare / clap Backbeat accent, punch and crack Beats 2 & 4 (4/4) All genres; claps common in trap
Hi-hat (closed) Rhythmic subdivision, keeps time Every 8th or 16th note Funk, R&B, lo-fi, hip-hop
Hi-hat (open) Sustain accent, "breath" in groove Before beat 2 or 4 Funk, soul, Afrobeats
808 bass Sub-bass melodic line, harmonic glue Tied to kick, after beat 1 Trap, drill, dark rap
Crash / ride Section marker, energy emphasis Beat 1 of a new section Rock, metal, jazz
Percussion Rhythmic texture, world influence Off-beats, in between snares Latin, Afrobeats, Dancehall

How to make beats online in 3 steps

From blank grid to finished beat in under a minute.

1
Set your BPM & time signature
Type in a tempo or tap it in rhythm. Choose 4/4 for hip-hop and house, 3/4 for waltz, or 6/8 for a triplet feel. The grid adjusts instantly.
2
Click pads to build your pattern
Tap each step to activate it. Start with kick on beats 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, and add hi-hats in between. Layer 808s for the bass line.
3
Export and use your beat
Hit Export and choose WAV or MP3. Download your beat and drop it straight into your DAW, share it to social media, or record vocals over it.

Frequently asked questions

Is this beat maker really free to use?
Yes — completely free. Open it in any browser, build a pattern, and export. No account required, no credit card, no time limit.
Do I need to install any software?
No installation needed. The beat maker runs entirely in your web browser using the Web Audio API. Works on Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, and on mobile devices.
What drum sounds are included?
The beat maker includes kick drums, snares, claps, hi-hats (closed and open), crash cymbals, 808 bass hits, percussion fills, and sub-bass patterns — plus the ability to upload your own samples.
What time signatures does the beat maker support?
You can work in 4/4, 3/4, 6/8, and 7/8. The step sequencer adjusts automatically so every time signature feels natural when you're building patterns.
What BPM range can I set?
You can set the BPM anywhere from 40 to 300. Tap tempo is also supported — just tap in rhythm and the beat maker locks to your speed.
Can I export my beat as an audio file?
Yes. You can export your beat as a WAV file (lossless) or MP3. The export renders all layers with your chosen BPM and effects applied.
Can I use beats I make here commercially?
Patterns you create are 100% yours. Any built-in samples are royalty-free for commercial use. Samples you upload remain yours under whatever license they already carry.
