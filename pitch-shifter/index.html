Frequently asked questions

Is the online pitch shifter really free?
Yes — Pocket's pitch-shifting tools are completely free to use in your browser. There is no account, no trial, and no download required.
How many semitones can I shift the pitch?
Pocket lets you shift pitch up or down by up to 12 semitones (one octave) in either direction. You can also fine-tune in cents for subtle pitch corrections.
Can I change the pitch without changing the tempo?
Yes. Pocket uses pitch-stretch algorithms that change pitch independently of playback speed, so your audio stays at the same tempo after shifting.
Does pitch shifting work on vocals?
Yes. You can shift the pitch of vocals to change the key of a song, transpose a vocal melody to fit a new chord progression, or apply subtle correction to tighten tuning. For best results on vocals, first isolate them with our free stem splitter.
What audio formats are supported?
Pocket processes audio that your browser can decode — typically MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG and FLAC. The pitch-shifted audio can be exported as a WAV file for use in any DAW or video editor.
Can I pitch-shift just the vocals and leave the music?
To pitch-shift only the vocals, first separate them from the music using the free stem splitter, then bring the vocal stem into Pocket for pitch adjustment.
Is there a way to find the key of my audio before shifting?
Yes — use the free BPM and key finder to detect the key of any audio file instantly, then use Pocket to shift it to your target key.
