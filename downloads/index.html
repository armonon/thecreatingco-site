Review your storage, quarantine selected files, and restore what you want to keep. You choose what goes.

Requires

Apple Silicon & Intel
Download

Download Reclaim for Mac

Free · No account required
Developer ID signed & Apple-notarized

This is an early release. Keep a backup and review each cleanup selection. Automatic permanent deletion is off unless you enable it.

THREE SIMPLE STEPS

From download to desktop.

  1. 01

    Open the DMG.

    Find Reclaim in your Downloads folder and open the disk image.

  2. 02

    Move to Applications.

    Drag Reclaim into Applications, then open it from there.

  3. 03

    Start small.

    Try disposable files first. Check quarantine and restore before reviewing important files.

Release details, checksum & support +

Clean-machine, physical Intel and oldest-macOS testing remain outstanding. This public alpha is not a fully qualified stable release. Emptying quarantine permanently deletes its contents.
