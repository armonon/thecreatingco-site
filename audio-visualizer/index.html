Why people use an audio visualizer

🎵 Understanding a mix
See exactly where the bass, mids and highs sit in a finished track — great for learning how a mix is constructed.
🎬 Music video production
Capture spectrum frames and reference the beat pattern when syncing visual cuts to a track in a video editor.
🎚️ Remixing and stem editing
Visualize individual stems to find the best edit points, see transients, and understand how each part contributes to the overall energy.
📚 Music learning and analysis
Students and educators use spectrum analyzers to visualize and explain frequency content, dynamics, and structure in recorded music.
🎤 Vocal checking
Visualize the vocal stem in isolation to spot resonance peaks, sibilance, or frequency clashes with other instruments.
🎧 DJ preparation
Analyze the frequency and energy profile of tracks before a set — understand how two tracks will combine in a mix.

Frequently asked questions

Is the audio visualizer free?
Yes — StemDeck's audio visualizer is completely free to use in your browser. No account, no download, and no trial limit.
What audio formats does the visualizer support?
StemDeck accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG, and most other audio formats your browser can decode. Just drop your file onto the player to start.
Does my audio file leave my browser?
No — StemDeck visualizes and processes your audio entirely in the browser. Your file is never uploaded to a server.
What visualizer modes are available?
StemDeck offers a real-time spectrum analyzer (frequency bars), a waveform display, and per-stem visualization — you can view the spectrum for vocals, drums, bass or melody independently.
Can I visualize just the vocals or just the drums?
Yes. StemDeck's stem separation lets you split a track and visualize each stem independently — solo the vocal stem, for example, to see just its frequency and amplitude in the visualizer.
Can I use this for a music video?
StemDeck is built for real-time visualization and remixing. You can capture visualizer frames as still images. For full music video rendering, pairing StemDeck with ScenePilot gives you a complete workflow.
Does the audio visualizer work on mobile?
Yes — StemDeck runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet, or phone. No installation needed on any device.
