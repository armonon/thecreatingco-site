OUR NEXT CHAPTER

A more useful internet.

More everyday tools. Better ways to connect your work. A gateway to free learning resources far beyond our own products.

That wider learning library and deeper cross-app continuity are goals, not finished services.

What connects today?

My Projects stores files and versions in this browser, with ZIP backup and restore. It is not cloud sync. Moving work between apps still means explicitly downloading and importing files.

NOW Messenger is a separate guest messaging app with its own identities and chats. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Read our privacy information.

OUR COMMITMENT

Creative freedom,
without a price of entry.

Lite stays free. Our commitment is a fully capable place to create, finish and keep your work.

Pro adds depth. We are building deeper, specialized workflows for people who need them. Lite should stand on its own.

Features, offline support and native or mobile releases vary by app and build. Check each product's current scope.

A NOTE FROM ARMON

I wanted more.
So I started building.

I’ve used computers my whole life. Different creative phases took me through After Effects, Photoshop, FL Studio, Blender and Cinema 4D, then djay Pro and Ableton. Different tools for different ways of making.

Along the way, I learned many of these apps deeply. I also kept noticing the features I wished they had—the things that could make creating easier, more powerful or more open.

Eventually, I realized I wanted more. I wanted the best tools I could imagine, while keeping them accessible to everyone. When I realized this was something I could build myself, I went all in. That’s what led me to thecreatingco.

— Armon Nasiri
