OUR STORY

Creativity shouldn’t
have an off switch.

We’ve spent years making images, music, films and 3D work. Along the way, we kept noticing the same friction: subscriptions, unreliable connections and workflows that interrupted the idea.

A missed bill shouldn’t mean a missed idea. Losing a connection shouldn’t mean losing momentum. We want artists to have more control over the tools they depend on.

That’s why we’re building thecreateco: a new generation of creative tools with freedom at the center.

LITE & PRO / OUR PROMISE

Creative freedom.
In both editions.

Making art should stay within reach. That belief shapes how we build Lite and Pro.

THECREATECO LITE

Always free.
Fully capable.

Lite will always remain free and fully capable—a complete place to create, finish and keep your work.

It’s built for real projects, with room to explore and grow. You should be able to keep making with Lite for as long as you want.

Our commitment: creative freedom, without a price of entry.
THECREATECO PRO

More depth.
When you need it.

Pro is where we’re building deeper workflows for more demanding projects and specialized ways of working.

Choosing Pro should be about the extra depth your work needs. Lite will continue to stand on its own as a fully capable creative tool.

Two editions. The same respect for your creativity.

WHERE WE’RE HEADING

Desktop in development. Mobile planned.

We’re developing Pro editions of the four Creative Suite apps for Windows and Mac, with iOS and Android editions planned for the App Store and Google Play. Reclaim for Mac is available as a free alpha; see Downloads & availability for current releases. Our goal is to give you more freedom to create on the devices you use every day.

Our public apps are still in alpha. Features, offline support and platform availability currently vary by product and build. See what’s available today ↗

A NOTE FROM ARMON

I wanted more.
So I started building.

I’ve used computers my whole life. Different creative phases took me through After Effects, Photoshop, FL Studio, Blender and Cinema 4D, then djay Pro and Ableton. Different tools for different ways of making.

Along the way, I learned many of these apps deeply. I also kept noticing the features I wished they had—the things that could make creating easier, more powerful or more open.

Eventually, I realized I wanted more. I wanted the best tools I could imagine, while keeping them accessible to everyone. When I realized this was something I could build myself, I went all in. That’s what led me to thecreateco.

— Armon Nasiri
