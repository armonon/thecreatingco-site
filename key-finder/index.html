Frequently asked questions

Is the key finder free?
Yes — StemDeck's key finder is completely free. No account, no download, and no usage limit.
How do I find the key of a song?
Upload the audio file or enter the notes you've identified, and the key finder analyses them to determine the most likely key and scale. It shows the root note, scale type (major, minor or mode), and the relative key.
What is a musical key?
A musical key is the tonal centre of a piece of music — the note and scale around which the melody and harmony are organised. A song in "C major" uses the notes C D E F G A B and resolves to C. Knowing the key helps you improvise, transpose, and understand chord progressions.
What is the difference between major and minor keys?
Major keys sound bright and resolved; minor keys sound darker and more tense. Every major key has a "relative minor" that shares the same notes — C major and A minor share all the same notes, but A minor centres on A. The key finder shows both interpretations.
Can the key finder detect modes?
Yes — the key finder supports all seven modes: Ionian (major), Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian (natural minor) and Locrian. If a track has a modal flavour, the finder will show the most likely mode alongside the parent key.
Why do I need to know the key of a song?
Knowing the key lets you choose the right scale for improvisation, transpose the song, find fitting chords, match a sample's key to your project, and understand the music theory behind a song.
Can I use the key finder on mobile?
Yes — the key finder works in any modern mobile browser. Upload a file or use the on-screen keyboard to enter notes and get the key instantly.
