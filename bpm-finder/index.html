Frequently asked questions

Is the BPM finder free?
Yes — StemDeck's BPM finder is completely free. No account, no download, and no usage limit.
How do I find the BPM of a song?
The easiest way is to tap along to the beat: open StemDeck, tap the BPM button on every beat for a few bars, and the tool averages your taps to show the BPM. You can also upload an audio file for automatic detection.
What is BPM?
BPM stands for Beats Per Minute — it measures the tempo of music. 60 BPM means one beat per second; 120 BPM means two beats per second. Most popular music falls between 80 BPM and 175 BPM depending on the genre.
Why do I need to know the BPM of a song?
Knowing the BPM lets you sync samples, loops and effects to the tempo of a track. DJs use it to beatmatch; producers use it to set a project tempo; dancers use it to count phrases; fitness instructors use it to match music energy to workout cadence.
How accurate is the BPM detection?
For tracks with a strong rhythmic beat — pop, hip-hop, EDM, rock — automated detection is typically accurate to within 1 BPM. Ambient or classical music with subtle rhythms may need manual tap input for best results.
Can I use the BPM finder on my phone?
Yes — the tap BPM input works on any device with a browser. Tap or click in time with the beat and the BPM is calculated from your tap intervals.
What audio formats does the BPM finder accept?
StemDeck accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG and most other formats your browser can decode.
