Frequently asked questions

Is the online audio recorder free?
Yes — completely free. Open the page, allow microphone access, press Record, and your audio starts capturing immediately. No account, no download, and no time limit on recordings.
What format will my recording be saved in?
You can download your recording as MP3, WAV, or OGG. WAV is lossless and best for further editing. MP3 is smaller and works everywhere. OGG is a free, open format supported in most modern browsers.
Can I record audio on my phone?
Yes — the online recorder works on mobile. Open the page in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone, allow microphone access, and tap Record. Downloads go to your phone's Downloads folder or Files app.
Is my recording kept on the server?
No — recording, processing, and downloading all happen locally in your browser. Your audio never leaves your device and is never sent to any server. The page has no upload step.
Can I pause and resume a recording?
Yes — the recorder supports pause and resume. Click Pause to stop capturing without ending the session. Click Resume to continue. The final download includes all segments joined as a single file.
What microphone quality should I use?
The recorder uses whatever microphone your device provides — laptop built-in, phone mic, USB condenser, or audio interface input. For the best quality, use an external microphone and record in a quiet room. The app records at 44.1 kHz, 16-bit by default.
