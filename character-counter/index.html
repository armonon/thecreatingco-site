Frequently asked questions

How many characters can a Twitter (X) post have?
Twitter (X) allows up to 280 characters per tweet for standard users. URLs count as exactly 23 characters regardless of their length. Spaces count toward the limit. X Premium subscribers can post longer articles, but standard tweets remain at 280 characters.
What is the Instagram caption character limit?
Instagram captions can be up to 2,200 characters. Only the first 125 characters are shown in the feed before a "more" link appears, so your hook should come first. Hashtags and emoji all count toward the 2,200 limit. Instagram allows up to 30 hashtags per post.
What is the LinkedIn post character limit?
LinkedIn posts support up to 3,000 characters. The first 210 characters (or around 3 lines) are visible in the feed before a "see more" button appears. LinkedIn articles can be up to 125,000 characters for long-form content.
How long should a meta description be for SEO?
Google typically displays 155–160 characters for desktop search results and slightly fewer on mobile. Keep your meta description under 160 characters to prevent it from being truncated with "…" in the SERP. There is no minimum, but 120–160 characters is the recommended range.
Does this counter count spaces as characters?
Yes — the "Characters" count includes spaces and all punctuation, matching how most social media platforms count. The "No spaces" count excludes spaces for comparison. Most platforms, including Twitter, count every space as one character.
How many characters is a word on average?
The average English word is approximately 5 characters. With a space added after each word, the average is around 5–6 characters per word. This means a 280-character Twitter post holds roughly 45–55 words. A 160-character SMS holds around 25–30 words.
What is the TikTok caption character limit?
TikTok captions can be up to 2,200 characters as of 2024 (increased from the original 300). Hashtags count toward the character limit. The TikTok bio is limited to 80 characters. Only a preview of the caption is shown in the For You page — tap "more" to see the full text.
