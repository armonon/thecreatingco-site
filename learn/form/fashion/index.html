The studio loads when you start. Your practice project stays in this browser.

Each fashion exercise checks actual pattern, seam, material and simulation state. The pendant session uses self-guided checks in Form’s Sketch and Model workspaces. Cloth simulation and fit estimates are experimental.

KEEP YOUR WORK

Real tools. Real project files.

Use Save inside the studio to keep your edited garment. The project menu also offers pattern SVG, apparel DXF, tiled PDF and a tech pack.

“Show me the tool” takes you to the relevant controls. Collapse the guide when you want more canvas space. Restart gives you a fresh practice project.
