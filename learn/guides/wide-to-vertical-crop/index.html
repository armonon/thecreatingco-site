How to calculate a vertical crop from a wide video

Turning a wide clip into a vertical frame discards much of the source. Work out the available width, then inspect subject movement across the whole clip.

Updated October 10, 2026 · by thecreatingco

Open ReframeApp overview & limits

A small example to work through

Our 1920 × 1080 source is cropped to 9:16 using its full height. The ideal crop width is 607.5 pixels, usually rounded to a practical whole-pixel width.

Try it and inspect the result

  1. Open Reframe and choose the intended output shape. Begin with a centre crop and identify the subject at the first frame.
  2. Watch the full clip, noting where the subject reaches the edges. Adjust the crop around action rather than around one attractive still.
  3. Review a small exported result if available. Confirm the output dimensions and inspect caption placement at the actual phone display size.

When the result differs

Subject disappears midway
Track movement through the clip and revise the framing; first-frame success is insufficient.
Text is clipped
Place text for the new frame rather than retaining landscape positions.
Need everything in frame
Use a layout with borders or a different composition; a crop cannot retain all source width.

What this example establishes

These dimensions are calculated from an example source, not Reframe output. Automatic framing is not a guarantee, and pixel rounding may differ by encoder. Judge the full motion and the final export.
