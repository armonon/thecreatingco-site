How to compare EQ changes without being fooled by volume

A louder version can seem better even when an EQ change damages the balance. Compare short passages at similar perceived loudness and keep the untreated version available.

Updated October 10, 2026 · by thecreatingco

Open BounceApp overview & limits

A small example to work through

Our original listening log uses three passages from a track you own: a quiet opening, a bass-heavy section and a dense ending. No audio processing results are claimed.

Original example worksheet
ItemExampleNotes
Opening0–10 sListen for hiss and vocal clarity
Bass section30–40 sListen for masking and low-end balance
Dense ending60–70 sListen for fatigue and harshness
Comparison levelSimilar perceived loudnessDo not choose solely because one is louder

Download the editable text worksheet · Download the CSV worksheet

Use the blank observation and decision fields to record your own result. These downloads are planning worksheets; they do not load an app project or demonstrate a completed export.

Try it and inspect the result

  1. Open your local audio in Bounce and listen with neutral EQ first. Note the passage you want to improve and what specifically bothers you.
  2. Change one band or a small group of bands, then compare at a similar playback level. Avoid making every part louder at once.
  3. Revisit all three passages. A setting that improves the bass section may make the opening thin; retain the original audio and your notes.

When the result differs

Change sounds better only at higher volume
Match listening level before deciding.
Player cannot decode a file
Try a supported alternative and keep the original; decoder support varies.
Playlist is missing on another device
Check the current storage behaviour; Bounce is a local-file player, not a streaming library.

What this example establishes

The worksheet is a listening method rather than an exported EQ curve or measured loudness analysis. Playback EQ does not necessarily create a new processed audio file. Confirm current controls and your actual file format.

This page supplies a worked example and a review method. It does not report a newly completed end-to-end app test. For current availability and edition details, read the Bounce overview.
