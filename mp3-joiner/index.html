Frequently asked questions

Is the MP3 joiner free to use?
Yes — completely free, with no account required and no file limits. Upload as many audio clips as you need, arrange them, and download the merged result.
What audio formats can I merge?
The joiner accepts MP3, WAV, OGG, FLAC, and M4A files. You can mix formats — for example, combine an MP3 and a WAV into one output file. Choose your output format (MP3 or WAV) before downloading.
Is there a gap or crossfade between the joined clips?
By default, clips are joined seamlessly with no gap. You can also set a crossfade duration (0.1 to 5 seconds) to smoothly blend the end of one clip into the start of the next — useful for music or podcast transitions.
Will joining audio reduce quality?
If you output as WAV, the merge is lossless — no quality loss. If you output as MP3, a re-encode step occurs. Use the highest bitrate option (320 kbps) to preserve as much quality as possible.
Are my files sent to a server?
No. All processing happens in your browser using the Web Audio API and WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device.
Can I reorder clips before joining?
Yes — drag and drop clips to reorder them before merging. The final joined file plays the clips in the order shown.
