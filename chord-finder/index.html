Frequently asked questions

Is the chord finder free?
Yes — StemDeck's chord finder is completely free. No account, no download, and no usage limit.
How do I identify a chord by its notes?
Enter the notes of the chord on the on-screen keyboard or by letter name and the finder identifies the chord name, type (major, minor, diminished, augmented, etc.) and any inversions. For example, entering C, E and G returns "C major".
Can the chord finder identify chords from audio?
Yes — you can play or upload a short audio clip and the chord recognition tool will analyse the frequencies to identify the most likely chord. Works best with clean, isolated chords on piano or guitar.
Does it show guitar chord diagrams?
Yes — for common chords, the finder shows guitar chord diagrams with finger positions and piano keyboard diagrams showing which keys to press.
What types of chords can it identify?
The chord finder covers major, minor, diminished, augmented, dominant 7th, major 7th, minor 7th, suspended, add9, and many extended chord types. It also identifies inversions and slash chords.
How do I find what key a chord progression is in?
Once you've identified each chord, look for the key where all the chords are diatonic. The chord finder shows which keys each chord belongs to — when several chords share a common key, that's likely the key of the progression.
Can I use the chord finder on mobile?
Yes — the chord finder works in any modern mobile browser. Tap notes on the on-screen keyboard or search by chord name.
