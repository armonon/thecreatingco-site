## Five Short Videos

### AutoCut
Openings: "Start with a song. Find the cut." / "What would this footage look like cut to the beat?" / "This is a first cut, not the last word."

Script: Import a real song and clips you own. Show analysis, play the first cut, lock a favorite shot, re-roll the rest and export. End: "Your first cut, then your decisions." Label browser export requirements; do not imply all music and phone recordings have been qualified.

### Split
Openings: "One song in. Four stems out." / "Nothing gets uploaded." / "What happens when you take the vocal out?"

Script: Explain that the initial model download is large. Run separation on owned or licensed audio, solo vocal and drums, then compare the original and instrumental. Download the stems and open the session in StemDeck. Keep audible separation artifacts in the demonstration rather than hiding them.

### Canvas
Openings: "Give your cover art a pulse." / "A visual loop that follows your track." / "Your next eight seconds of artwork."

Script: Add an owned track and cover image, compare two scenes, change the palette and export a vertical loop. Show the actual downloaded file playing. For a Spotify Canvas example, export silent video and verify the current submission requirements separately.

### Glyph Studio
Openings: "What would your handwriting look like as a font?" / "Draw a letter. Then make a family." / "The space between two letters changes everything."

Script: Draw A and V, compare spacing before and after a kerning adjustment, type a word and export TTF. Show a saved .glyph project. Say "TTF" explicitly for the kerning demonstration; do not claim OTF preserves it.

### Captions
Openings: "Make this clip understandable without sound." / "Start with subtitles. Keep your voice." / "The transcript is a draft. You get the final word."

Script: Use a short owned spoken clip, generate text, correct one recognition mistake, apply a style and download subtitles or a supported burn-in export. Show the correction; do not advertise perfect transcription.
