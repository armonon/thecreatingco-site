Common questions

Stem splitting, answered.

Is this stem splitter really free?
Yes. Split is free to use in your browser with no account or payment required. The separation model downloads once to your device and runs locally on subsequent uses.
Does my audio get uploaded to a server?
No. Split runs its AI model locally in your browser after a one-time model download (~172 MB). Your audio is never sent to any server — everything happens on your device.
Which stems can I separate?
Split separates audio into four stems: vocals, drums, bass and other (everything else, including melodic instruments and synths). You can mute and solo each stem in the browser before downloading.
What audio formats does it accept?
Split accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG and most common audio formats supported by your browser. Output stems are downloaded as WAV files.
How long does separation take?
The first run downloads the separation model (~172 MB), which can take a few minutes on slower connections. After that, a typical 3–4 minute track separates in under a minute on a modern computer. CPU-only machines will be slower than those with GPU support.
Can I use the stems for remixing?
Yes — Split includes a direct link to open your stems in StemDeck, thecreatingco's browser mixing studio. You can balance stem layers, build an arrangement, add effects and export a mix — all free, all in the browser.
