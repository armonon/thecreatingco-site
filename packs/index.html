FREE PACKS

Free packs. Made with our own tools.

Real files, made entirely with thecreateco's own apps — a LUT pack from Grade, starter patterns for Pocket, and a display font drawn in Glyph Studio. Everything here is ours to give away: no samples, no stock, no third-party content. Download starts immediately; no email required.

COLOR · MADE WITH GRADE

thecreateco LUT Pack

Nine original color looks — Warm Film, Cool Night, Bleach Bypass, Faded Matte, Teal & Orange, Mono Contrast, Golden Hour, Soft Pastel and Deep Shadow — built with our own parameterized color-grading math (matrix mixing, tone curves, split toning), not borrowed LUTs.

  • 9 looks · .cube, 33³
  • 2.1 MB
  • CC BY 4.0 thecreateco

Pick a look and drop its .cube file into ScenePilot's Grade tool or Browser Lite's Auto Director → Look LUT import — it's a standard .cube file, so it also opens in Premiere, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut through a LUT utility.

SOUND · MADE WITH POCKET

Pocket Starter Patterns

Eight original 16-step grooves — house, boom-bap, trap, dembow, UK garage, lo-fi, minimal techno and afrobeat-influenced — written directly against Pocket's own pattern format, ready to drop into a session and bend into your own.

  • 8 patterns · JSON
  • 8 KB
  • CC BY 4.0 thecreateco

Pocket saves patterns to your browser only, so there's no one-click import yet: open Pocket, open your browser console, paste the one-line command included in the pack, and all eight grooves show up in your saved list, ready to load and make your own.

ALSO CONSIDERED

What we left out, and why.

Canvas visual presets — skipped. Canvas (Sattari's audio-reactive looping-visuals tool) lets you pick a scene and a palette live in the app, but it has no preset file format to save, export or import — a "preset" isn't a thing you can download for it today. Rather than invent a fake import feature, we're skipping this item. If Canvas grows real preset export, this pack gets a fourth download.

HONEST NOTES

What to expect.

Current limitations

These are public alpha tools and a first programmatic type release. LUTs are a universal, portable format (.cube) — they work in Grade, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut (via a LUT utility) and most other editors, not only ours. Pocket patterns load through a browser-console paste, not a one-click import, because Pocket itself has no import UI yet. The font is drawn and compiled entirely in code with Glyph Studio's own engine — it has no hand-kerning pairs in this first release. Everything here is CC BY 4.0 thecreateco: use it, remix it, ship it, just keep the credit.
