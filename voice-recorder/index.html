Frequently Asked Questions

How do I record my voice online for free?

Open the voice recorder in your browser, click Record, and allow microphone access when prompted. Your browser captures audio directly from your mic — nothing is uploaded. Click Stop when done, then Download to save your recording.

Does the voice recorder work without installing anything?

Yes. The recorder uses the Web Audio API and MediaRecorder API built into every modern browser. There is nothing to install — no app, no extension, no plugin.

What file format will my recording be saved in?

You can download as MP3 or WAV. MP3 gives smaller files (great for sharing). WAV gives lossless quality (better for further editing). Typical voice recordings are around 1 MB per minute in MP3.

Is my voice recording stored on a server?

No. Everything stays in your browser tab. Your voice is captured by your microphone, held in browser memory, and saved directly to your device when you click Download. No recording ever touches a server.

Can I pause and resume a recording?

Yes. Tap Pause to hold without stopping the timer or losing what you've recorded, then Resume to continue. The final download includes everything before and after the pause.

What microphone should I use for the best voice quality?

Any built-in laptop or phone mic works fine for voice memos. For clearer recordings — podcasts, voiceovers, presentations — a USB condenser mic like the Audio-Technica AT2020 or a Bluetooth headset with a boom mic reduces background noise significantly.

How long can I record?

There is no hard time limit — you can record as long as your device has available RAM. In practice, recordings under 60 minutes are completely smooth. Very long sessions (2+ hours) may slow down older devices.
