Frequently asked questions

Is the GIF maker free?
Yes — completely free. Upload a video or image sequence, adjust the settings, and download your GIF. No account, no watermark, no file size limit.
What video formats can I convert to GIF?
The GIF maker supports MP4, WebM, MOV, and AVI video files. You can also create a GIF from a sequence of JPG or PNG images uploaded in order.
Is my video uploaded to a server?
No — the GIF maker processes everything in your browser using the Canvas API and WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
Can I control GIF frame rate and size?
Yes — you can set the frame rate (5–30 fps), resize the output dimensions, and adjust the color palette (16–256 colors). Lower frame rates and smaller sizes produce smaller files that load faster and share more easily.
How do I make a GIF from a video clip?
Upload your video, trim the section you want using the start and end handles, set the frame rate and size, then click Create GIF. The animated GIF downloads automatically to your device when conversion is complete.
Can I make a GIF from images?
Yes — upload multiple JPG or PNG images in the order you want them animated and the GIF maker will use them as frames. Set the delay between frames to control the animation speed.
Can I use the GIF maker on my phone?
Yes — the GIF maker is fully mobile-optimised. Open it in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone, upload a video from your camera roll, and download the GIF to share directly from your phone.
Why is my GIF file so large?
GIF files store uncompressed frames, so they can be large. To reduce file size: trim the clip to under 4 seconds, reduce the output width to 480px or less, lower the frame rate to 10–12 fps, and reduce the color count to 128 or below.
