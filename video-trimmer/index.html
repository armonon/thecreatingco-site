Frequently Asked Questions

Can I trim a video online without uploading it?

Yes. This video trimmer works entirely in your browser using the File API and WebCodecs. Your video is never sent to any server — it stays on your device the whole time.

What video formats can I trim online?

MP4 (H.264/H.265), MOV, WebM, and AVI are supported. MP4 is the most widely compatible format for trimming and re-downloading.

Will trimming re-encode my video and reduce quality?

For many trims, the tool can perform a fast seek-and-cut that avoids full re-encoding — quality stays identical to the source. When the cut point falls between keyframes, a short segment is re-encoded at high quality; the difference is imperceptible.

Is there a maximum file size for the video trimmer?

The limit depends on your browser and device RAM. Most modern devices handle files up to 4 GB without issues. Very large files (10 GB+) may be slower on older hardware.

Can I trim a video to an exact timestamp?

Yes. You can type an exact time (HH:MM:SS.mmm) into the start and end fields, or drag the handles on the timeline scrubber for visual trimming.

What is the output format after trimming?

Output defaults to MP4, which is compatible with all major platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, iMessage, etc.). WebM output is available for web-native workflows.

Can I trim multiple clips from one video?

Yes. Set your in/out points and add the clip to a queue, then set different in/out points for the next segment. All clips can be downloaded individually or merged into one continuous export.
