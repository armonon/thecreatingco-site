Format Type Trim quality Best use

Frequently asked questions

Is the audio trimmer free?
Yes — completely free. Upload your audio file, set the start and end points, and download the trimmed clip. No account, no watermark, no time limit.
What audio formats does the trimmer support?
The audio trimmer works with MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AAC, and WebM files. You can download the trimmed result as MP3 or WAV.
Does trimming reduce audio quality?
No — the trimmer uses lossless cutting for WAV and FLAC files, meaning only the audio outside the selected range is removed; the audio inside is not re-encoded. For MP3 files, lossless cutting is possible at the frame boundary level. Choose WAV output to preserve the full original quality.
Can I trim an MP3 file online?
Yes — just upload your MP3, drag the start and end handles on the waveform to select the section you want to keep, and click Download. The trimmer cuts the MP3 without re-encoding so quality is preserved.
Is my audio file uploaded to a server?
No — the audio trimmer processes your file entirely in the browser using the Web Audio API. Your file is never uploaded to any server. It never leaves your device.
Can I use the audio trimmer to make a ringtone?
Yes — that's one of the most popular uses. Upload a song, trim it to 20–30 seconds around the hook or chorus, then download as MP3 to add to your phone as a custom ringtone.
Can I trim audio on my phone?
Yes — the audio trimmer is fully mobile-optimised. Open it in your phone browser, upload a file from your phone storage, set the trim points, and download. Works on iPhone (Safari) and Android (Chrome).
